10/21, 10/28Social & Emotional Skills
Wednesday: Being Perceived as Friendly Daily Check-in Ask students what traits they look for in a friend Allow them to answer verbally Do you think being kind is important in friendships? What are some benefits of being friendly? Allow students to answer verbally Next Slide: Read the reasons why being friendly is important People will want to be your friend People like kindness in friends It makes people more likely to help you If you are kind to people, they typically want to be kind back, people do not want to help you if you are mean to them It is a good skill to have at a job Being kind can help you make a good first impression, a boss will see that you can be kind to both customers and coworkers It makes you feel better When you are kind to people, it releases serotonin which is a chemical hormone that makes you feel happy Gives you more opportunities People may offer you jobs, trips, invites to events/parties Look at Being perceived as Friendly worksheet: read it together as a class “This worksheet talks about ways you can be perceived as friendly if you don't know where to start” There is nothing to fill out on this worksheet, students should just have it and we can talk about it as a class Sometimes saying the right things can take thought. Make sure you think about if what you are about to say is kind and respectful Is it an inside thought or a thought you should say out loud Inside vs outside thoughts Hand out cards and have students answer whether they are inside or outside thoughts Are they kind, can the person fix the problem right away, is it helpful Have students put their cards on the board in the think it or say it column Ask students what they can do to be perceived as friendly, and answer as a quick check on Canvas Work on missing assignments
