Dealing with Disappointment

10_20-10_27 Dealing with Disappointment.docx

10/20, 10/27Social & Emotional Skills
Tuesday: Dealing with Disappointment Daily Check-In What’s your favorite Halloween candy? Group activity: Healthy Self Expression Read sheet (on board) Have the students come up and color in the boxes with healthy self expression and explain why they filled that box in Ask students in what ways do they express their feelings (there is no pressure for them to share this out loud) Introduce lesson: dealing with disappointment with video Show video When plans change, it can be disappointing, disappointment can feel like anger, it is important that we express our feelings in a healthy way when we feel like this Dealing with Disappointment worksheet Read it together and then ask students to fill out at the bottom a time they were disappointed by something (they can share if they'd like) Read next slide about how disappointments help us grow Remind students of our perseverance lesson: it is important to not give up when we feel badly about something, continuing during a hard or disappointing time helps us grow Have students fill out the “Disappointments help us grow” paper: What advice would you give a friend dealing with disappointment? Read summary slide Its okay to feel upset when there are disappointments Staying flexible helps you accept and adapt to unexpected changes Disappointment can feel like anger, it is important that you recognize how you are feeling and chose a healthy way to express yourself Choose a coping mechanism that helps you: taking a walk, using a fidget, talking to a friend/teacher Good things can come out of disappointment: Maybe a fun trip gets canceled, but another one that is even more fun gets scheduled for a different time Work on missing assignments
