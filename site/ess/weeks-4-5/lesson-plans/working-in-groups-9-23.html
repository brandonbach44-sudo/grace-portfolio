Working in Groups

Working in Groups 9_23.docx

09/23, 09/30Social & Emotional SkillsEssential Skills and Strategies (ESS)

PA Standards

  • PA SEL Competency — Relationship Skills: Use communication and social skills to interact effectively with others, including active listening, turn-taking, and staying on topic during group interactions.
  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Analyze the importance of collaboration and group participation skills in school, work, and community settings.

Essential Questions

  • Why is it important to be able to work well in groups?
  • What does working well in a group look like in school, at work, and in the community?

Objectives

  • Students will identify key group-participation skills: listening, sharing ideas, not talking over others, and staying on topic.
  • Students will connect the conversation ball activity to real-life group work skills.
  • Students will identify examples of situations that require working in groups in school, at work, and in the community.
  • Students will share a personal experience related to working in a group.

Assessment

  • Participation in the conversation ball activity, including turn-taking and responding to follow-up questions
  • Verbal identification of the group skills used during the activity
  • Participation in the discussion of school/work/community group examples
  • Sharing of a personal group-work experience

Differentiation

  • Visual schedule posted and referenced for predictability
  • Conversation ball provides a physical, turn-based cue so students know when it's their turn to speak
  • Teacher models follow-up questions to scaffold conversational engagement for students who need support extending their answers
  • Concrete, real-world examples (school/work/community) used to make the group-work concept concrete rather than abstract
  • Option to pass during personal sharing
  • Choice of homework or a game during work time to accommodate individual pacing/needs

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed and referenced at the start of class
  • Consistent daily routine: check-in → Wordle → conversation ball → group skills lesson → discussion → work time
  • Group norms reinforced during the conversation ball: one person talks at a time, listen to whoever is holding the ball, ask follow-up questions
  • Positive reinforcement for using group skills (listening, staying on topic) during discussion

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • (0-5 min) Students enter, follow the schedule on the board, and complete their daily check-in.
  • (5-15 min) Wordle completed together as a group.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule
  • Daily check-in sheet or digital tool
  • Wordle (device/board)
  • Conversation ball
  • Discussion prompts/examples for school, work, and community group scenarios
  • Homework materials and/or games (Uno, Monopoly, etc.) for end-of-period work time

Vocabulary

  • Listening — paying attention to what someone else is saying
  • Staying on topic — saying something that connects to what's already being discussed
  • Collaboration — working together with others toward a shared goal

Lesson Sequence

  • (15-25 min) Group engagement activity: pass around the conversation ball. Each student answers the question their thumb lands on; everyone gets a turn. The class asks follow-up questions to each person's answer.
  • (25-35 min) Transition into the lesson: explain that the conversation ball activity was practice for working in groups, since students listened, shared their ideas, didn't talk over their classmates, and stayed on topic.
  • (35-45 min) Review examples of when working in groups is needed: in school, at work, and in the community.
  • (45-55 min) Students share a personal experience of a time they needed to work in a group.

Closure

  • (55-90 min) Work time: students choose to work on homework or play a game (Uno, Monopoly, etc.).
  • Briefly recap that everyone practiced the same group skills today (listening, sharing, staying on topic) that they'll need in school, at work, and in the community.
