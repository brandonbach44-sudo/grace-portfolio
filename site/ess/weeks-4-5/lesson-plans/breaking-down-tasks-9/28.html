1. Check-In / Warm-Up (as planned) 2. Transition into Mindfulness Monday: "Alright, it's Mindfulness Monday, which means we're starting the day with a short video to help clear our minds. Remember last week, when we talked about emotional awareness? We talked about how noticing your feelings gives you a chance to choose your response instead of just reacting. This meditation is one of the tools we can use to actually get to that calm, clear-headed place. Let's watch it together." (Play 5-minute meditation video) 3. Transition into the Group Task-Breakdown Activity: Put up slides with breaking down task practice on them and ask students to verbally break down tasks into small parts Let's start with making a peanut butter and jelly sandwich. What's the very first thing you'd need to do?" (Take responses, build out the full list of steps together — getting bread, opening the jar, spreading, etc.) "Now let's try a different kind of task, choosing an outfit for the day. What are all the steps involved in that?" (Take responses again, building out the list.) Let students know they just broke down these tasks into smaller parts and today we will be talking about breaking tasks down into smaller parts 4. Slide 1 Purpose: "So why do we bother breaking tasks down into steps? A few reasons: It turns something BIG into small, easy steps It can make projects, homework, or tasks feel easier It helps you manage your time Basically, when a task feels overwhelming, breaking it into steps is one of the best tools we have to make it feel doable again." "Let's watch a short video that shows some tips and strategies for how to actually do this." (Play video on breaking tasks down) 5. Transition into Tips Discussion: "So we just saw some strategies in that video — let's talk about a few of our own that can really help: Make yourself small due dates Set yourself timers Only focus on one step at a time Make yourself a checklist Which of these do you think would help you the most? Has anyone already tried one of these without realizing it had a name?" (Take a few responses/discussion.) 6. Transition into Canvas Quick Check: "Now it's your turn to try this on your own. You have a quick check on Canvas, pick any activity or task of your own choosing, and break it down into steps, just like we did with the sandwich and the outfit." 7. Transition into Homework Time:
