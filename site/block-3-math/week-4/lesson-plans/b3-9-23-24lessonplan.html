03/09Learning Support Math

PA Standards

CC.2.2.HS.D.9 / CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Evaluate algebraic expressions by substituting given values for variables and applying order of operations, including expressions with absolute value.

Essential Questions

  • How do we evaluate algebraic expressions using substitution and order of operations?
  • How does absolute value affect the value of an expression?

Objectives

  • Students will practice foundational math skills independently via IXL for progress monitoring.
  • Students will evaluate algebraic expressions using substitution and order of operations.
  • Students will evaluate expressions involving absolute value.
  • Students will demonstrate understanding of evaluating expressions, absolute value, and order of operations through practice and game-based review.

Assessment

  • IXL progress-monitoring data
  • Whiteboard responses during the warm-up (informal check for understanding)
  • Gimkit performance data
  • Completed practice worksheet (absolute value, order of operations, evaluating expressions)
  • Participation and performance during review Jeopardy

Differentiation

  • Visual schedule and visible timer displayed during IXL to support pacing and predictability
  • Whiteboards used during the warm-up for low-stakes practice and quick teacher checks
  • Calculators permitted throughout for computation support
  • Gimkit provides a gamified format with immediate feedback
  • Jeopardy played in teams/pairs to reduce individual pressure, with the option to confer before answering
  • Reduced problem set or extended time available on the practice worksheet as needed

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's segments, including the lunch break
  • Timer displayed during IXL to support time awareness
  • Whiteboards, markers, and calculators distributed efficiently before the warm-up begins
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions between each activity
  • Positive, team-based structure established for the review Jeopardy

Activating Prior Knowledge

(0-15 min) IXL for progress monitoring, with the visual schedule and a timer displayed on the board.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Timer
  • Devices for IXL
  • Whiteboards, dry-erase markers, calculators
  • Gimkit set on evaluating expressions (device access)
  • Practice worksheet: absolute value, order of operations, and evaluating expressions
  • Review Jeopardy game (board/slides)

Vocabulary

  • Evaluate — to find the value of an expression
  • Substitute — replacing a variable with a given number
  • Absolute value — the distance a number is from zero, always positive
  • Order of operations (PEMDAS) — the order in which operations must be performed

Lesson Sequence

  • (15-25 min) Warm-up: 4 evaluating-expressions questions completed together on the board. Students follow along using whiteboards and calculators.
  • (25-32 min) Gimkit review game on evaluating expressions.
  • (32-45 min) Practice worksheet covering absolute value, order of operations, and evaluating expressions.
  • LUNCH ---
  • (45-90 min) Review Jeopardy covering evaluating expressions, absolute value, and order of operations for the remainder of class.

Closure

  • Wrap up Jeopardy with a quick recap of the most commonly missed questions/concepts.
  • Collect the practice worksheet.
  • Preview the next lesson or upcoming assessment on these topics.
