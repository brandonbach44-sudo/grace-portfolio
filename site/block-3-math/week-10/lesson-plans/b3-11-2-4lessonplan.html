Review & Quiz: Distributive Property, Variables on Both Sides, Multistep

B3_11-2_4LessonPlan.docx

11/02, 11/04Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.2.HS.D.9: Use reasoning to solve equations and justify the solution method.
  • CC.2.2.8.B.3: Analyze and solve linear equations.

Essential Questions

  • How does the distributive property help me solve an equation?
  • What do I do when there are variables on both sides of an equation?
  • How can I check that my answer is correct?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Solve equations with variables on both sides using inverse operations.
  • Use the distributive property to simplify expressions and solve equations.
  • Solve multistep equations by carrying out each step in the correct order.
  • Show what they know about all three skills on a quiz.

Assessment

  • Formative: Student participation in the whole-class warm-up.
  • Formative: Review worksheet, with my observation and help as I circulate.
  • Formative: Observation of partners during the distributive roll-a-dice game.
  • Summative: Quiz on the distributive property, variables on both sides, and multistep equations.

Differentiation

  • Content: I show examples of each skill on the board before students begin the review.
  • Process: I walk around the room to give help as each student needs it.
  • Process: Students practice the distributive property with a partner in a game.
  • Choice: Students may quietly take a break, work on other work, or finish their multistep color-by-number from the previous class.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students begin with the warm-up on the whiteboard together.
  • Students work independently on the review while I circulate to help.
  • Students who finish the review hand it in and wait for everyone else, then play the distributive roll-a-dice game with a partner.
  • We continue the game after lunch, then students take the quiz.
  • Students who finish the quiz stay quiet and take a break or work on other work, such as their color-by-number.

Activating Prior Knowledge

The warm-up on variables on both sides reviews a skill students have practiced. The review sheet and the examples on the board bring back multistep equations, variables on both sides, and the distributive property before the quiz. The roll-a-dice game makes the practice fun.

Materials Needed

Whiteboard and markers, warm-up equations, review worksheet, distributive roll-a-dice game (dice and game sheets), quiz, multistep color-by-number from the previous class, pencils

Vocabulary

Variable; equation; distribute; distributive property; inverse operation; coefficient; constant; solution; check

Lesson Sequence

  • Warm-Up (whole class)
  • We work on a variables on both sides warm-up together on the whiteboard.
  • Review
  • The review has multistep equations, variables on both sides, and distributive property questions.
  • I show examples of each type on the board.
  • Students work on the review while I walk around the room to help.
  • Distributive Roll-a-Dice Game
  • After every student finishes the review and hands it in, students play the distributive roll-a-dice game with a partner.
  • Lunch
  • After Lunch
  • We continue the distributive roll-a-dice game.
  • Students take a quiz on the distributive property, variables on both sides, and multistep equations.
  • When finished, students quietly take a break or work on other work, including finishing their multistep color-by-number from the previous class if they haven’t already.

Closure

  • As students finish the quiz, they hand it in and quietly take a break or work on other work. I use the quiz results to see which skills students know and which need more practice.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Teacher uses the quiz results to plan the next lesson.
