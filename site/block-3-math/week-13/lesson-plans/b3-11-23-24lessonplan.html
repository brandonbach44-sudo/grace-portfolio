B3_11-23_24LessonPlan

03/11Learning Support Math

PA Standards

CC.2.2.8.B.2: Understand the connections between proportional relationships, lines, and linear equations, including slope and y-intercept.

Essential Questions

  • How can I find the slope and y-intercept from an equation in slope-intercept form?
  • What do the m and the b mean in y = mx + b?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Identify the slope and y-intercept from an equation in slope-intercept form.
  • Find the matching slope and y-intercept on their bingo card and cover it with a bingo chip.
  • Complete missing assignments.

Assessment

  • Formative: Student bingo cards: whether students match each called equation to the correct slope and y-intercept. I check every bingo that is called.
  • Formative: Observation and questions during bingo.
  • Practice data: Missing assignments completed.

Differentiation

  • Supports: The visual schedule on the projector shows the plan for the day.
  • Content: Students use bingo cards with the answers already on them, so they match instead of writing.
  • Process: A random number generator and prizes keep the game fair and fun, and we play multiple rounds so students get more practice.
  • Process: Students catch up on missing assignments at their own pace, with my help.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Class is shortened to 52 minutes for the half day.
  • A visual schedule on the projector shows the plan: slope-intercept bingo, then missing assignments.
  • Students cover spaces with bingo chips, and I check any bingo that is called.
  • Students get a prize for a correct bingo.
  • I help students who need it as they catch up on missing assignments.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students are practicing slope-intercept form, and a bingo game gives them a fun way to practice reading the slope and y-intercept from equations. Because it is a half day, the plan is simple, with time to catch up on missing assignments.

Materials Needed

Visual schedule (projector), random number generator (projector), 48 numbered calling cards with equations, bingo cards with 16 spaces, bingo chips, prizes, missing assignments, pencils

Vocabulary

Slope; y-intercept; slope-intercept form; equation; bingo; across; down; diagonal; chip

Lesson Sequence

  • Arrival — 52-minute class
  • Students see the visual schedule on the projector.
  • The plan today is to play slope-intercept bingo and catch up on missing assignments.
  • Slope-Intercept Bingo (multiple rounds until class is over)
  • Each student gets a bingo card with 16 spaces and bingo chips.
  • To win a round, a student needs 4 in a row: across, down, or diagonal.
  • I have 48 numbered calling cards. Each card has an equation in slope-intercept form (for example, y = 4x + 5).
  • A random number generator is on the projector. I call out the equation on the card with that number.
  • Students look at their card to see if they have the matching slope and y-intercept (for y = 4x + 5, slope 4 and y-intercept 5).
  • If they have it, they place a bingo chip over the space.
  • When a student gets bingo, they call it and I check their card against the equations called.
  • A student with a correct bingo gets a prize, and we play another round.
  • Missing Assignments
  • Students also work on catching up on missing assignments, as part of the plan for the day.

Closure

  • At the end of the last round, I ask a volunteer to explain how they found the slope and y-intercept in one equation. I use the bingo checks to see who needs more practice.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students continue practicing slope-intercept form.
