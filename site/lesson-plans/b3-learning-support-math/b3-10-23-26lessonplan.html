Distributive Property & Variables on Both Sides

B3_10-23_26LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 3 Math/Week 9/Lesson Plans, Block 3 Math/Week 8/Lesson Plans

10/23, 10/26Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Apply properties of operations, including the distributive property, to expand and simplify linear expressions.
  • CC.2.2.8.B.3 / 8.EE.C.7 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Solve linear equations in one variable, including equations with variables on both sides.

Essential Questions

  • How does the distributive property help us simplify an expression?
  • How do we solve an equation when there are variables on both sides?

Objectives

  • Students will simplify expressions using the distributive property in a warm-up.
  • Students will finish practicing the distributive property.
  • Students will take guided notes on solving equations with variables on both sides and work through the examples.

Assessment

  • Distributive property warm-up responses (4 questions)
  • Completed distributive practice
  • Completed variables on both sides notes and examples

Differentiation

  • Warm-up displayed on the board and provided on paper so students can follow along in the format that works best for them
  • Warm-up and notes examples completed together before independent work
  • Distributive practice finished first so students build confidence with the previous skill before new content begins
  • Teacher support available throughout the practice and notes

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's activities
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions between activities
  • Consistent routine carried over from the previous distributive property lessons

Activating Prior Knowledge

(0-10 min) Distributive property warm-up: 4 questions displayed on the board, with a paper version for each student. The class works through the questions together.

Materials Needed

  • Distributive property warm-up (on the board and paper copies, 4 questions)
  • Distributive practice (from the previous class)
  • Variables on both sides guided notes
  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Pencils, calculators

Vocabulary

  • Distributive property — review
  • Variable — a letter that represents an unknown or changing number
  • Inverse operations — review
  • Like terms — review

Lesson Sequence

  • (10-45 min) Finish the distributive practice, with teacher support available as needed.
  • (45-85 min) Variables on both sides notes: go through the notes and examples together as a class.

Closure

  • (85-90 min) Collect the distributive practice and notes.
  • Briefly recap the main idea of solving equations with variables on both sides and preview that the next lesson will continue it.
