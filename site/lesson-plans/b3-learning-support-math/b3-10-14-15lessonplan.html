Combining Like Terms: Maze & Review

B3_10-14_15LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 3 Math/Week 7/Lesson Plan

10/14, 10/15Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Apply properties of operations to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions, including combining like terms.

Essential Questions

  • What makes two terms 'like terms'?
  • How do we combine like terms to simplify an expression?

Objectives

  • Students will simplify expressions by combining like terms in a warm-up.
  • Students will identify and define the parts of a term: coefficient, variable, exponent, and constant.
  • Students will review and correct a combining like terms practice worksheet from the previous class.
  • Students will practice combining like terms using a math maze and additional practice problems.
  • Students will practice foundational math skills independently via IXL for progress monitoring.
  • Students will review combining like terms through a Gimkit game.

Assessment

  • Responses to the 5 combining like terms warm-up problems
  • Completed vocabulary fill-in (coefficient, variable, exponent, constant)
  • Corrections/participation while reviewing the previous worksheet
  • Progress and accuracy on the combining like terms maze
  • IXL progress-monitoring data
  • Completed additional combining like terms practice
  • Gimkit performance data

Differentiation

  • Materials list and daily schedule posted on the opening slide so students know what to gather and what to expect
  • Vocabulary slide uses a color-coded example (4x² + 5) with each part labeled, giving a concrete visual reference for coefficient, variable, exponent, and constant
  • Previous worksheet reviewed together before new independent work begins
  • Maze offers a more engaging, lower-pressure format than a standard worksheet
  • 15-minute timer displayed during IXL to support pacing
  • Gimkit provides a gamified format with immediate feedback
  • Teacher support available during all independent practice

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Opening slide displays the materials needed (calculator, pencil, math folder, laptop) and the schedule for the day
  • Calming background music video played during independent practice to support focus
  • Visible 15-minute timer displayed during IXL
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions, especially around the lunch break

Activating Prior Knowledge

(0-10 min) Students gather their materials and follow the schedule on the board. Warm-up: 5 combining like terms problems on the slides (e.g., 2x + 3 + 4x - 6), followed by a vocabulary review using the color-coded example 4x² + 5 (coefficient, variable, exponent, constant).

Materials Needed

  • Slideshow: materials/schedule slide, warm-up problems, vocabulary slide, practice slide, IXL timer slide, Gimkit slide
  • Calculator, pencil, math folder, laptop (for each student)
  • Vocabulary fill-in sheet (coefficient, variable, exponent, constant)
  • Combining like terms practice worksheet from the previous class
  • Combining like terms maze
  • Additional combining like terms practice
  • 15-minute timer video (for IXL)
  • Calming background music video (for independent practice)
  • Combining like terms Gimkit

Vocabulary

  • Coefficient — the number directly next to the variable
  • Variable — the letter (e.g., x, y, a, b) in an equation or expression
  • Exponent — the small, raised-up number
  • Constant — the number that is on its own and does not change in value
  • Like terms — review

Lesson Sequence

  • (10-25 min) Review the combining like terms practice worksheet from the previous class together.
  • (25-45 min) Pass out the combining like terms maze; students begin working on it before lunch.
  • LUNCH ---
  • (45-60 min) 15 minutes of IXL for progress monitoring, with the timer video displayed.
  • (60-75 min) Students complete additional combining like terms practice, with calming background music playing and teacher support available.
  • (75-82 min) As students finish, go over the practice worksheet together.
  • (82-90 min) Play a combining like terms Gimkit for extra review.

Closure

  • Collect the maze and practice work (students may finish the maze in a future class if needed).
  • Briefly recap how to identify and combine like terms.
