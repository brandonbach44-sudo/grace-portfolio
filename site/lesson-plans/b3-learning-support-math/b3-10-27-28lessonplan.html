Variables on Both Sides & Multistep Equations

10/27, 10/28Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.2.8.B.3 / 8.EE.C.7 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Solve linear equations in one variable, including equations with variables on both sides and multistep equations.
  • CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Apply properties of operations to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions, including combining like terms.

Essential Questions

  • How do we solve an equation when there are variables on both sides?
  • How can combining like terms help us solve multistep equations?

Objectives

  • Students will solve equations with variables on both sides in a warm-up and small group practice.
  • Students will practice foundational math skills independently via IXL recommendations.
  • Students will review combining like terms through a Gimkit game.
  • Students will begin taking notes on solving multistep equations.

Assessment

  • Whiteboard responses during the variables on both sides warm-up
  • Accuracy and progress on the variables on both sides small group practice
  • IXL recommendations progress
  • Gimkit performance data
  • Multistep equations notes started in class

Differentiation

  • Visual schedule lists the materials needed (whiteboard, marker, calculator) so students arrive prepared
  • Whiteboards give a low-stakes way to show work during the warm-up
  • Small groups with one teacher per group allow individualized support and pacing
  • IXL recommendations give students who finish early personalized practice at their own level
  • Gimkit provides a gamified, low-pressure review with immediate feedback
  • Multistep equations notes begin only after review and practice of prior skills

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's activities and the materials students need (whiteboard, marker, calculator)
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions, especially into small groups and around the lunch break
  • Two teachers, one leading each small group, provide support throughout the work time
  • Clear expectation that students who finish early log into IXL recommendations independently

Activating Prior Knowledge

(0-8 min) Students enter, see the visual schedule listing the materials they need (whiteboard, marker, calculator), and complete a variables on both sides warm-up using their whiteboards.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Whiteboards, dry-erase markers, calculators
  • Variables on both sides warm-up
  • Variables on both sides practice materials (for two small groups)
  • Devices for IXL and Gimkit
  • Combining like terms Gimkit
  • Multistep equations guided notes

Vocabulary

  • Variables on both sides — review
  • Like terms — review
  • Inverse operations — review
  • Multistep equation — an equation that takes more than two steps to solve (new)

Lesson Sequence

  • (8-43 min) Split into two small groups, with one teacher per group, to work on variables on both sides. When or if students finish, they log into IXL and work on their recommendations.
  • (43-45 min) Wrap up and transition to lunch.
  • LUNCH ---
  • (45-60 min) 15 minutes of IXL recommendations.
  • (60-70 min) Combining like terms Gimkit review.
  • (70-90 min) Begin multistep equations notes together as a class.

Closure

  • Before lunch: review main idea of variables on both sides
  • Briefly recap the main idea of multistep equations and preview that the next lesson will continue them.
