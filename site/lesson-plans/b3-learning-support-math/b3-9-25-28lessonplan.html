Radicals, Rational & Irrational Numbers, Test Review

B3_9-25_28LessonPlan.docx

← Evaluating Expressions & Absolute ValueUnit 1 Review & Test →
09/25, 09/28Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.1.8.E.4 / 8.NS.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Distinguish between rational and irrational numbers.
  • CC.2.1.8.E.1 / 8.EE.A.2 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Simplify expressions involving square roots (radicals).
  • CC.2.2.HS.D.9 / CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1: Apply order of operations to evaluate expressions.
  • Note: verify alignment with individual students' IEP math goals.

Essential Questions

  • How do we simplify radical expressions?
  • How can we classify numbers as rational or irrational?
  • How does order of operations help us evaluate expressions correctly?
  • What strategies can we use to prepare for an upcoming test?

Objectives

  • Students will simplify radical (square root) expressions.
  • Students will classify numbers as rational or irrational.
  • Students will apply order of operations to evaluate expressions.
  • Students will complete a test review packet mirroring the test format to prepare for the upcoming assessment.

Assessment

  • Participation and performance in the review Jeopardy
  • Whiteboard/verbal responses during the simplifying radicals warm-up
  • Gimkit performance data (rational/irrational number game and order of operations game)
  • Completed test review packet

Differentiation

  • Test review packet mirrors the actual test format/directions to build familiarity and reduce test anxiety
  • Gimkit provides a gamified format with immediate feedback for both review topics
  • Brain break video included to support attention and self-regulation mid-period
  • Teacher circulates and chunks the review packet into sections for students needing extra support or reduced workload
  • Reference sheet/notes available during packet work as appropriate to individual accommodations

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's segments, including the lunch break and brain break
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions between activities
  • Consistent routine carried over from the prior lesson (Jeopardy continues from where it left off)
  • Positive, low-pressure tone maintained during test review to reduce anxiety

Activating Prior Knowledge

(0-20/25 min) Finish the review Jeopardy game from the previous lesson.

Materials Needed

  • Jeopardy review game (continued from previous lesson)
  • Whiteboards, dry-erase markers, calculators
  • Devices for Gimkit (rational/irrational numbers game and order of operations game)
  • Test review packet (formatted to mirror the actual test)
  • "Would You Rather" Halloween edition brain break video
  • Projector

Vocabulary

  • Radical — a root expression (e.g., a square root)
  • Simplify — writing an expression in its simplest form
  • Rational number — a number that can be written as a fraction of two integers
  • Irrational number — a number that cannot be written as a fraction; a non-repeating, non-terminating decimal
  • Order of operations (PEMDAS) — review

Lesson Sequence

  • (8 min) Warm-up: 2 simplifying radicals questions completed together.
  • (7 min) Gimkit: rational/irrational numbers review game.
  • (remaining time until lunch) Students work on the test review packet, formatted to look just like the upcoming test.
  • LUNCH ---
  • Students finish the test review packet.
  • (5 min) Brain break: "Would You Rather" Halloween edition video.
  • (7 min) Gimkit: order of operations review game.

Closure

  • Collect the completed test review packet.
  • Briefly recap any commonly missed concepts from Jeopardy, the warm-up, or the Gimkit games.
  • Preview the upcoming test and remind students what to bring/review beforehand.
