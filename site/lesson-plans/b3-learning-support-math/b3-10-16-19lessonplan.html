Quiz & Intro to the Distributive Property

10/16, 10/19Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.2.7.EE.B.4 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Solve real-life and mathematical problems using numerical and algebraic equations, including two-step linear equations.
  • CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Apply properties of operations to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions, including combining like terms and the distributive property.
  • Note: verify alignment with individual students' IEP math goals.

Essential Questions

  • How do we solve two-step equations and combine like terms accurately?
  • What does the distributive property allow us to do with an expression?

Objectives

  • Students will solve two-step equations in a warm-up and guided review.
  • Students will practice two-step equations in a small group setting.
  • Students will review combining like terms through a Gimkit game.
  • Students will demonstrate their understanding of two-step equations and combining like terms on a quiz.
  • Students will begin taking notes on the distributive property.

Assessment

  • Two-step equation warm-up responses
  • Participation and accuracy during the two-step review and small group practice
  • Gimkit performance data
  • Two-step equations and combining like terms quiz
  • Distributive property notes started in class

Differentiation

  • Two-step equations reviewed whole class and again in small groups before the quiz, scaffolding from supported to independent
  • Small groups led by two teachers (lead teacher and co-teacher) for more individualized support and pacing
  • Gimkit provides a gamified, low-pressure review with immediate feedback
  • Quiz accommodations provided per IEP as needed (e.g., questions read aloud, extended time, calculator use)
  • Distributive property notes begin only after the quiz, so new content does not compete with quiz preparation

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's activities, including the lunch break and quiz
  • Clear transition cues when moving into small groups and into quiz time
  • Calm, quiet, focused environment established for the quiz, with expectations reviewed beforehand
  • Two teachers in the room allow small groups and individualized support

Activating Prior Knowledge

(0-10 min) Two-step equation warm-up.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Two-step equation warm-up problems
  • Two-step equation review materials
  • Small group practice materials (one set per group)
  • Devices for Gimkit
  • Two-step equations and combining like terms quiz
  • Distributive property guided notes
  • Pencils, calculators

Vocabulary

  • Two-step equation, inverse operations — review
  • Like terms, coefficient, variable, constant — review
  • Distributive property — multiplying a number by each term inside parentheses (new)

Lesson Sequence

  • (10-20 min) Two-step equation review together as a class.
  • (20-37 min) Split into two small groups to complete the practice work: one group works with the lead teacher and the other works with the co-teacher.
  • (37-45 min) Combining like terms Gimkit review.
  • LUNCH ---
  • (45-70 min) Students complete the two-step equations and combining like terms quiz, with accommodations as needed.
  • (70-90 min) Begin distributive property notes together as a class.

Closure

  • Collect quizzes.
  • Briefly recap what the distributive property is and preview that the next lesson will continue it.
