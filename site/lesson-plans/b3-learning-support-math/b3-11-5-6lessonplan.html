03/11, 05/06Learning Support Math

PA Standards

CC.2.1.7.D.1: Analyze proportional relationships and use them to solve real-world and mathematical problems.

Essential Questions

  • What is a proportion?
  • How can I use a proportion to find a missing number?
  • How can I check that my answer makes sense?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Copy and use notes on proportions.
  • Solve proportion problems with support, starting with examples done together.
  • Complete a proportions practice worksheet.

Assessment

  • Practice data: IXL recommendations during the first 15 minutes.
  • Formative: Student answers as we go through the notes questions together on the board.
  • Formative: Practice worksheet on proportions, with my observation and help as I circulate.

Differentiation

  • Supports: The visual schedule and timer show students what comes next.
  • Content: Notes are on the board for students to copy, so everyone has the same notes.
  • Process: We go through the notes questions together on the board and do the first few worksheet problems together.
  • Process: I circulate to help the students who need it.
  • Process: IXL recommendations are personalized to each student.
  • Environment: Students get a 5-minute break before lunch.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • The visual schedule is on the board when students enter, so they know they start with 15 minutes of IXL.
  • A timer on the board shows how much IXL time is left.
  • Students copy notes from the board, and we go through the questions together.
  • Students take a 5-minute break before lunch.
  • After lunch we finish the notes and start the practice worksheet together, then students work while I circulate.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students start with IXL, which they know well. The notes and the questions we do together on the board introduce proportions, and the first few worksheet problems are done together so students can see the steps before they try on their own.

Materials Needed

Visual schedule, board, timer, student devices for IXL, proportion notes, paper and pencils, proportions practice worksheet

Vocabulary

Ratio; proportion; equivalent; cross multiply; missing number; check

Lesson Sequence

  • Arrival and IXL — 15 minutes
  • The visual schedule is on the board, and students see they start with 15 minutes of IXL.
  • Students work on their IXL recommendations with a timer on the board.
  • Proportion Notes
  • Transition to proportion notes.
  • The notes are on the board and students copy them.
  • We go through the questions together on the board.
  • Break — 5 minutes before lunch
  • Lunch
  • After Lunch
  • We finish the notes.
  • We go through the proportions practice worksheet. We do the first few problems together.
  • Students continue the worksheet while I circulate to help those who need it.

Closure

  • At the end of class, I ask students to tell me what a proportion is and one step they used to solve one. I look at the worksheets to see who needs more practice.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students continue practicing proportions.
