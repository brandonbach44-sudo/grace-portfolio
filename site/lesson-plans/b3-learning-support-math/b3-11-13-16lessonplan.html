03/11Learning Support Math

PA Standards

CC.2.2.8.B.2: Understand the connections between proportional relationships, lines, and linear equations, including slope as a rate of change.

Essential Questions

  • What is slope, and what does it tell me about a line?
  • How can I tell if a slope is positive, negative, zero, or undefined?
  • How can I use the slope formula to find the slope between two points?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Explain rate of change and identify the four types of slope: positive, negative, zero, and undefined.
  • Use Slope Man to remember the types of slope.
  • Copy and use the slope formula notes, with examples done together.
  • Begin independent slope practice, if time allows.

Assessment

  • Formative: Student answers as we do the examples together on the board.
  • Formative: Student responses naming the types of slope.
  • Formative: Independent slope practice, if we get to it.

Differentiation

  • Content: Slope Man gives students a visual way to remember positive, negative, zero, and undefined slope.
  • Content: Notes are on the board for students to copy, so everyone has the same notes.
  • Process: We do examples together after each set of notes.
  • Process: Independent practice is only given if there is time.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students begin Unit 2 with notes on the board, which they copy.
  • We do the examples together after each set of notes.
  • Students go to lunch after the first examples, and we continue with the slope formula afterward.
  • Independent practice is given only if time allows.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students have seen proportions and rates, so rate of change builds on what they already know. Slope Man gives them a fun picture for remembering the four types of slope before they use the slope formula.

Materials Needed

Board, rate of change and types of slope notes, Slope Man, slope formula notes, examples, paper and pencils, independent slope practice (if time allows)

Vocabulary

Slope; rate of change; positive; negative; zero; undefined; rise; run; slope formula; point

Lesson Sequence

  • Rate of Change and Types of Slope Notes
  • We start Unit 2, slope, with notes on rate of change and the types of slope.
  • I show students Slope Man to help them remember positive slope, negative slope, zero slope, and undefined slope.
  • We do the examples together.
  • Lunch
  • After Lunch
  • Students take notes on the slope formula.
  • We do examples together.
  • If we get to it, students begin independent slope practice.

Closure

  • At the end of class, I ask students to name the four types of slope and tell me one step of the slope formula. I use their answers to see who needs more practice.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students continue practicing slope.
