03/11, 09/10Learning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.1.7.D.1: Analyze proportional relationships and use them to solve real-world and mathematical problems.
  • Absolute value: Understand absolute value as the distance of a number from zero on a number line.

Essential Questions

  • How can I use a proportion to find a missing number?
  • What does absolute value tell me about a number?
  • How can I check that my answer makes sense?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Complete their IXL benchmark and practice their IXL recommendations.
  • Solve proportion problems with support, beginning with warm-up questions done together.
  • Copy absolute value notes and work through examples together.
  • Complete a proportions practice worksheet independently.

Assessment

  • Diagnostic: IXL benchmark.
  • Practice data: IXL recommendations.
  • Formative: Student answers on the proportions warm-up as we go through it together.
  • Formative: Student answers on the absolute value examples.
  • Formative: Proportions practice worksheet, with my observation and help as I circulate.

Differentiation

  • Time: Students take as much time as they need on the benchmark.
  • Process: Students who finish the benchmark work on their IXL recommendations until everyone is done.
  • Content: Students get a paper copy of the warm-up questions to follow along with the board.
  • Process: We do the examples on the notes together on the board.
  • Process: I circulate to help during the worksheet.
  • Small group/individual support: My cooperating teacher pulls students one by one for extra help with proportions.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students enter and begin their IXL benchmark right away, with as much time as they need.
  • Students who finish work quietly on IXL recommendations until everyone is done.
  • We do the proportions warm-up together on the board, and students follow along on paper.
  • Notes are continued after lunch.
  • During the worksheet, I circulate and my cooperating teacher works with students one at a time.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students begin with IXL, which they already use. The proportions warm-up reviews a skill students have seen, and doing it together on the board helps them see each step. The warm-up gets students ready for the proportions worksheet later in the lesson.

Materials Needed

Student devices for IXL, proportions warm-up (4 questions, paper copies), board, absolute value notes, proportions practice worksheet, paper and pencils

Vocabulary

Ratio; proportion; equivalent; cross multiply; absolute value; distance; zero; number line

Lesson Sequence

  • IXL Benchmark
  • Students come in and take their IXL benchmark.
  • They have as much time as they need.
  • When they finish, they work on their IXL recommendations until everyone is done.
  • Proportions Warm-Up — about 8–10 minutes
  • We do 4 proportion questions together on the board.
  • Each student gets a paper copy of the questions to follow along.
  • Absolute Value Notes
  • We begin the absolute value notes.
  • We do the examples on the notes together on the board.
  • Lunch
  • After Lunch
  • We continue the absolute value notes and the examples on the board.
  • Students work individually on a proportions practice worksheet.
  • I circulate to help.
  • My cooperating teacher pulls students one by one for extra help with proportions.

Closure

  • At the end of class, I ask students to tell me what absolute value means and one step they used to solve a proportion. I look at the worksheets to see who needs more practice.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students continue practicing proportions and absolute value.
