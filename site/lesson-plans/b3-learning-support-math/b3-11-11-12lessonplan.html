03/11, 11/12Learning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.1.7.D.1: Analyze proportional relationships and use them to solve real-world and mathematical problems.
  • Absolute value: Understand absolute value as the distance of a number from zero on a number line.

Essential Questions

  • How do I solve a proportion?
  • How do I solve an absolute value equation?
  • How can I check that my answer makes sense?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Finish the proportions practice worksheet.
  • Solve proportion equations and absolute value equations with support during the review.
  • Review absolute value and proportions by playing Gimkit.
  • Show what they know about proportions and absolute value on the quiz.

Assessment

  • Formative: Proportions practice worksheet.
  • Formative: Quiz review: questions we do together and questions students do independently.
  • Formative: Gimkit results on absolute value and proportions.
  • Summative: Quiz on proportions and absolute value.

Differentiation

  • Time: Students who haven’t finished the proportions worksheet get time to finish it.
  • Process: We do a few review questions together and students do a few independently.
  • Process: Gimkit gives students a fun way to practice and get quick feedback.
  • Process: A short Gimkit review on proportions after lunch helps students get ready for the quiz.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students begin by finishing their proportions practice worksheet if they haven’t already.
  • Students work on the review together and then independently.
  • The class plays Gimkit before lunch and after lunch.
  • After lunch, students have the rest of class to take the quiz.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students have practiced proportions and absolute value in earlier lessons. The review and the Gimkit games bring those skills back before the quiz, and Gimkit makes the practice fun.

Materials Needed

Proportions practice worksheet, quiz review (4 proportion questions and 4 absolute value questions), Gimkit (absolute value and proportions), student devices, quiz, paper and pencils

Vocabulary

Ratio; proportion; cross multiply; absolute value; distance; zero; solve; check

Lesson Sequence

  • Finish the Proportions Practice Worksheet
  • Students finish the worksheet if they haven’t already.
  • Quiz Review
  • The review has 4 proportion questions and 4 absolute value questions.
  • We do a few together and students do a few independently.
  • Absolute Value Gimkit — about 10 minutes
  • Students play an absolute value Gimkit.
  • Lunch
  • After Lunch
  • We play a short Gimkit review on proportions.
  • Students take the quiz and have the rest of class to finish it.

Closure

  • As students finish the quiz, they hand it in. I use the quiz and Gimkit results to see which skills students know and which need more practice. This finishes our Unit 1 material.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Teacher uses the quiz results to plan the next lesson and the start of the next unit.
