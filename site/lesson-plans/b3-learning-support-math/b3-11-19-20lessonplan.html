03/11Learning Support Math

PA Standards

CC.2.2.8.B.2: Understand the connections between proportional relationships, lines, and linear equations, including slope and y-intercept.

Essential Questions

  • How can I use the slope formula to find the slope between two points?
  • How can I write an equation in slope-intercept form from a slope and a y-intercept?
  • How can I graph a line from its equation?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Practice slope skills on IXL.
  • Use the slope formula with two coordinates to find the slope.
  • Complete a slope riddle by finding the slope in each problem.
  • Write an equation in slope-intercept form from a slope and a y-intercept, and graph it on graph paper.
  • Review slope-intercept form by playing Gimkit.

Assessment

  • Practice data: IXL practice during the first 15 minutes.
  • Formative: Slope riddle worksheet: the answer at the bottom shows whether students found the slopes correctly.
  • Formative: Whiteboard equations and graphs during the flash card groups.
  • Formative: Gimkit results on slope-intercept form.

Differentiation

  • Supports: The visual schedule is on the board, so students know the plan for the day.
  • Process: IXL practice gives each student questions at their own level.
  • Content: I show examples of the riddle on the board before students work independently.
  • Small groups: After lunch, students split into two groups, one with me and one with my cooperating teacher.
  • Time: Students may finish the riddle after lunch if they need more time.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • The visual schedule is on the board when students enter.
  • Students start with 15 minutes of IXL practice.
  • Students work independently on the slope riddle after IXL.
  • After lunch, students are in two small groups, one with me and one with my cooperating teacher.
  • The class ends with a slope-intercept Gimkit.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students have learned the slope formula and slope-intercept form. IXL gives each student practice at their own level, and the riddle gives independent practice with a built-in check at the end. After lunch, writing equations and graphing them connects slope and y-intercept to a line, and Gimkit finishes the day with review.

Materials Needed

Visual schedule (board), student devices for IXL, slope riddle worksheet, whiteboards, markers, erasers, calculators, slope-intercept flash cards (slope and y-intercept), graph paper, pencils, Gimkit (slope-intercept), student devices

Vocabulary

Slope; slope formula; coordinates; y-intercept; slope-intercept form; graph; equation

Lesson Sequence

  • Arrival and IXL Practice — 15 minutes
  • The visual schedule is on the board.
  • Students do 15 minutes of IXL practice.
  • Slope Riddle
  • We move into the slope riddle. I show examples of the riddle on the board.
  • Students continue independently.
  • At the end, students should get the answer to the riddle at the bottom of their worksheet.
  • Students can finish after lunch if needed.
  • Lunch
  • After Lunch: Slope-Intercept Flash Cards — 30–35 minutes
  • Students split into two groups, one with me and one with my cooperating teacher.
  • Students grab a whiteboard, marker, eraser, and calculator.
  • We have flash cards with a slope and a y-intercept on them.
  • Students write the slope-intercept equation based on the flash card, and then graph the equation on graph paper.
  • Slope-Intercept Gimkit
  • We use the rest of class to play a slope-intercept Gimkit.

Closure

  • At the end of class, the Gimkit shows which students know slope-intercept form and which need more practice. I look at the IXL data, riddle answers, equations, and graphs to see who needs more help.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students keep practicing slope and slope-intercept form.
