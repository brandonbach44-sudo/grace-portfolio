Combining Like Terms

B3_10-7_8LessonPlan.docx

← Two-Step Equations in Small GroupsLike Terms: UNO & Practice →
10/07, 10/08Equations & AlgebraLearning Support Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.2.7.EE.A.1 (foundational skill per IEP goals): Apply properties of operations to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions, including combining like terms.
  • CC.2.2.7.EE.B.4: Solve real-life and mathematical problems using numerical and algebraic equations, including two-step linear equations.

Essential Questions

  • How do we combine like terms to simplify an expression?
  • How does solving two-step equations build on what we know about combining like terms?

Objectives

  • Students will practice foundational math skills independently via IXL for progress monitoring.
  • Students will solve two-step equations as a warm-up/review.
  • Students will review vocabulary: like terms, coefficient, variable, constant, exponent.
  • Students will take guided notes and work through examples on combining like terms.
  • Students will visually identify sets of like terms in class examples by marking them with squares and circles.
  • Students will review combining like terms through a Gimkit game.
  • Students will begin independent practice on a combining like terms worksheet.

Assessment

  • IXL progress-monitoring data
  • Two-step equation warm-up responses
  • Vocabulary review responses
  • Completed guided notes on combining like terms
  • Accuracy marking squares/circles on like terms in class examples (4-5 problems)
  • Gimkit performance data
  • Progress on the independent practice sheet

Differentiation

  • Visual schedule and timer displayed during IXL to support pacing and predictability
  • Visual marking system (squares for one set of like terms, circles for the other) gives students a concrete way to sort terms before combining them
  • Guided notes and examples completed together as a class before independent work begins
  • Gimkit provides a gamified format with immediate feedback
  • Teacher support available as students begin the independent practice sheet

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule and timer displayed during IXL
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions between activities
  • Positive reinforcement during the squares/circles board activity and Gimkit review

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • (0-15 min) IXL for progress monitoring, with the visual schedule and a timer displayed on the board.
  • (15-25 min) Two-step equation warm-up.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Timer
  • Devices for IXL
  • Two-step equation warm-up sheet
  • Vocabulary review materials
  • Combining like terms guided notes
  • Board example problems for the squares/circles marking activity
  • Devices for Gimkit
  • Combining like terms practice sheet

Vocabulary

  • Like terms — terms that have the same variable raised to the same power
  • Coefficient — the number multiplied by a variable
  • Variable — a letter that represents an unknown or changing number
  • Constant — a number with no variable attached
  • Exponent — review

Lesson Sequence

  • (25-30 min) Vocabulary review.
  • (30-50 min) Combining like terms notes and examples, completed together as a class.
  • (50-62 min) Practice: with an example problem on the board, students place a square on one set of like terms and a circle on the other set (4-5 problems).
  • (62-69 min) Combining like terms Gimkit review.
  • (69-90 min) Students begin a combining like terms independent practice sheet.

Closure

  • Collect any completed independent practice work.
  • Briefly recap how to identify and combine like terms.
