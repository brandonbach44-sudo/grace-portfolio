09/28Money & BankingLife Skills Math (Money Monday)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Analyze consumer skills, including budgeting and determining affordability of items, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Math: Add multiple prices to determine a total cost and compare it to a given budget.

Essential Questions

  • How do we determine if we have enough money to buy an item or items?
  • How can I use my communication device to express my understanding of money?

Objectives

  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will use their AAC device to indicate whether they can afford an item (yes/no) or state its price, given a $3 budget, across 4 grocery catalog slides.
  • Group 1: Students will participate in a mindfulness meditation video and follow class expectations modeled using their AAC device.
  • Group 2: Students will add the prices of 3 grocery items and determine whether all 3 can be purchased within a $15 budget.
  • Both groups: Students will complete daily progress monitoring.

Assessment

  • Group 1: AAC device responses (yes/no or stated price) across the 4 Money Monday catalog slides
  • Group 1: progress monitoring data
  • Group 2: completed price addition/budget check for the 3 grocery items
  • Group 2: progress monitoring data
  • Observation of following the daily routine and expectations

Differentiation

  • Group 1: AAC device vocabulary (yes/no, item prices) modeled by the teacher before independent use
  • Group 1: mindfulness video and class expectations modeled using the AAC device to reinforce communication
  • Group 1: catalog slides use pictures alongside prices, with low, simple prices for single-item comparison
  • Group 2: task is more complex (adding 3 prices against a budget), matched to their higher independence level
  • Group 2: visible timer displayed during snack/break to support predictability
  • Visual schedule and materials list posted for both groups

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual routine and materials list (math folder, laptop, pencil) posted on the board
  • Group 1 greeted individually at the start of their block, modeled with the AAC device
  • Timer displayed on the board during Group 2's snack/break
  • Clear transition between groups
  • Calm, patient environment maintained during AAC modeling and mindfulness video

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users (0-45 min):
  • (0-5 min) Students enter, follow the routine on the board, and gather their materials (math folder, laptop, pencil). Greet the first group of students, modeled with the AAC device.
  • (5-10 min) Mindfulness meditation video.
  • (10-15 min) Review class expectations, modeled using the AAC device.

Materials Needed

  • Visual routine and materials list (board/projector)
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • Mindfulness meditation video
  • Money Monday grocery catalog — Group 1 version: 4 slides, $3 budget, one item per slide with picture and price
  • Money Monday grocery catalog — Group 2 version: list of 3 items with prices, $15 budget
  • Timer (Group 2 snack/break)
  • Math folders, laptops, pencils
  • Progress monitoring tool/program

Vocabulary

  • Afford — having enough money to buy something
  • Budget — the amount of money you have to spend
  • Price — how much an item costs
  • Total — the sum after adding amounts together

Lesson Sequence

  • (15-25 min) Money Monday warm-up: view the 4 grocery catalog slides. For each slide ("You have $3, can you afford ___?"), students use their AAC device to say yes/no or state the item's price.
  • (25-45 min) Progress monitoring for Group 1.
  • GROUP 2 — no AAC devices (45-90 min):
  • (45-60 min) Snack and break (15 min), with a timer displayed on the board.
  • (60-65 min) Students enter, follow the routine on the board, and gather their materials.
  • (65-80 min) Money Monday warm-up: students view a catalog showing 3 items with prices and determine whether they can buy all 3 with a $15 budget by adding the prices together.
  • (80-90 min) Progress monitoring for Group 2.

Closure

  • Briefly recap: did you have enough money for your items today? What helped you figure that out?
  • Preview that next Monday's Money Monday warm-up will continue building on these budgeting skills.
