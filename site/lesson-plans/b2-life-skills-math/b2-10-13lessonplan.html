Time Tuesday & Taking Inventory

B2_10-13LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 2 Math/Week 7/Lesson Plan

10/13Time & Measurement · Counting, Number Sense & DataLife Skills Math (Time Tuesday)

PA Standards

  • Functional Math: Tell time on a clock and count inventory accurately, skills used in daily living and work settings.
  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional life skills needed for independent living and employment.

Essential Questions

  • How do we tell time using a clock?
  • How do we count and keep track of inventory, and why does it matter in everyday life and at work?
  • How can mindfulness help us start class calm and focused?

Objectives

  • Students will participate in a mindfulness meditation to start class calm and focused.
  • Students will tell time on a clock at their individual level during the Time Tuesday warm-up.
  • Students will count inventory accurately.
  • Students will complete daily progress monitoring.

Assessment

  • Participation in the meditation
  • Accuracy telling time on the clock during the warm-up
  • Accuracy counting inventory
  • Progress monitoring data

Differentiation

  • Clock and inventory tasks matched to each student's individual level
  • Manipulative clocks and AAC device supports available for students who use them
  • Visual schedule and routine posted for predictability
  • Adult support available throughout the warm-up and progress monitoring

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual routine and materials list posted on the board
  • Calm, quiet environment maintained during the meditation
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions between activities

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • Students enter, follow the routine on the board, and gather their materials.
  • Mindfulness meditation to start class.

Materials Needed

  • Visual routine and materials list (board/projector)
  • Mindfulness meditation video
  • Clocks (teacher demonstration clock and individual student clocks)
  • Inventory items and counting sheet
  • Progress monitoring tool/program
  • AAC devices as needed

Vocabulary

  • Clock, hour, half hour — review
  • Inventory — the items a store or business has on hand
  • Count / total — review

Lesson Sequence

  • Time Tuesday warm-up, part 1: students work with clocks, showing and telling the time at their individual level.
  • Time Tuesday warm-up, part 2: students count inventory and record their counts.
  • Progress monitoring for the day.

Closure

  • Briefly recap what was practiced today: telling time and counting inventory.
  • Preview that next Tuesday's Time Tuesday warm-up will continue building on these skills.
