10/15Life Skills Math (Bankability Prep)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Analyze consumer and personal finance skills, including checking account balances, counting money, and completing a deposit slip, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Math: Use a calculator to add and subtract; count money and record amounts.

Essential Questions

  • How can a calculator help us solve addition and subtraction problems?
  • How do we keep track of our money and prepare to make a deposit at the bank?

Objectives

  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will participate in a mindfulness meditation, learn the ASL sign of the week, and review class expectations.
  • Group 1: Students will use a calculator to solve addition and subtraction problems in 'Monster Math.'
  • Students will log into their Bankability accounts, record their account balance, and decide whether they want to shop at Bankability on Friday.
  • Students will count their money and fill out a deposit slip to bring to the bank the next day.

Assessment

  • Group 1: participation in meditation, sign of the week, and expectations review
  • Group 1: accuracy using the calculator to solve Monster Math problems
  • Accuracy recording the account balance from Bankability
  • Shopping decision circled (yes/no) for Friday
  • Accuracy counting money and completing the deposit slip

Differentiation

  • Group 1: calculator used for Monster Math so students can focus on choosing the operation and entering numbers, supported by AAC device use as needed
  • Monster Math game format increases engagement through a goal (collecting keys to escape the mansion)
  • Deposit slip and Bankability sheet modeled step-by-step before independent work
  • Adult support available for logging in, counting money, and filling out the deposit slip
  • Visual schedule and consistent routine maintained for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's routine for each group
  • Group 2 has a snack break upon entering, consistent with established routine
  • Calm, quiet environment maintained during the meditation
  • Clear transition between Group 1 and Group 2 blocks

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users:
  • (5 min) Mindfulness meditation.
  • (5 min) ASL sign of the week introduced and demonstrated.
  • (5 min) Read the class expectations together.
  • (10 min) Warm-up: 'Monster Math.' Students use a calculator to solve addition and subtraction problems in order to collect all the keys and get out of the Monster Mansion.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • Mindfulness meditation video
  • ASL sign of the week reference
  • Monster Math game (addition and subtraction problems)
  • Calculators
  • Devices with Bankability access
  • Bankability account sheet (record account amount; circle yes/no for shopping Friday)
  • Money to count and deposit slips

Vocabulary

  • Deposit — adding money into an account (review)
  • Deposit slip — the form completed to bring money to the bank
  • Balance — the amount of money in an account
  • Add / subtract — review

Lesson Sequence

  • (20 min) Lesson, Group 1: prepare for Bankability. Students log into their Bankability accounts, write down how much is in their account, and circle whether they want to shop at Bankability on Friday. Students then count their money and fill out the deposit slip they will bring to the bank the next day.
  • GROUP 2 — no AAC devices:
  • (15 min) Snack and break upon entering.
  • (10 min) Warm-up routine.
  • (20 min) Lesson, Group 2: prepare for Bankability, following the same steps as Group 1 (log in, record account amount, circle yes/no for shopping Friday, count money, and fill out the deposit slip).

Closure

  • Briefly recap: how much is in your account, and are you shopping on Friday?
  • Remind students to bring their deposit slip and money to the bank tomorrow.
