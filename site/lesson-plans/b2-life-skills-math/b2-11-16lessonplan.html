02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Math skills: Match pictures of money to their amounts, and count and name how many of each icon.
  • Functional skills: Prepare food for a class meal by peeling and cutting potatoes with adult support.
  • IEP goals: Students communicate in any way that works for them, including pointing, drawing, speaking, or using their talker, following each student’s IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How can I show that I know how much money is shown?
  • How can I count and tell how many I see?
  • How can I help prepare food for our class luncheon?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Group 1: Match pictures of money (1, 2, and 5 dollars) to the amounts on the whiteboard, and count the icons on a fall counting worksheet and tell how many using their talker or another way.
  • Group 2: Help prepare for Friday’s luncheon by peeling potatoes with adult support, throwing away their peels, and cutting the potatoes into four if they are comfortable.
  • Both groups: Use the ASL sign of the week, “cheap.”

Assessment

  • Group 1: Student matches of money pictures to amounts, shown by drawing a line, pointing, or using a talker.
  • Group 1: Student counts of each icon on the fall counting worksheet, shown with their talker or another way.
  • Group 2: Observation of potato peeling and cutting with adult support.
  • Both groups: Observation of communication during the daily routine.

Differentiation

  • Group 1: All communication works: drawing a line, pointing, or using a talker.
  • Group 1: Students can work at their seats or on the whiteboard.
  • Group 2: Adults stay near students to help while they peel potatoes.
  • Group 2: Cutting the potatoes into four is optional, for students who feel comfortable.
  • Group 2: Group 2 takes a snack and break first.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Group 1 follows the usual routine: mindfulness, expectations, and the ASL sign of the week.
  • Group 1 may respond by drawing, pointing, or using their talker, so every student can take part.
  • Group 2 begins with a snack and break and then goes to the kitchen.
  • Adults stay near students in the kitchen, and students throw away their peels.
  • The potatoes are for the luncheon on Friday.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students use the daily routine and practice money and counting skills in new ways. Group 2 helps prepare food for the Friday luncheon, which gives them a real purpose for the activity.

Materials Needed

Mindfulness, classroom expectations, ASL sign of the week (“cheap”), whiteboard and markers, pictures of money (1, 2, and 5 dollars) and amounts, fall counting worksheets, students’ talkers, snack items, potatoes, peelers, knives, bowls, trash can for peels

Vocabulary

Cheap; money; dollar; count; match; potato; peel; cut; luncheon

Lesson Sequence

  • Group 1
  • Mindfulness.
  • Expectations.
  • ASL sign of the week, “cheap.”
  • Money matching warm-up on the whiteboard: students see pictures of money (small amounts: 1, 2, and 5 dollars) and match each picture to the amount.
  • Students can draw a line to connect them on the whiteboard, point, or use their talker. All communication works.
  • Fall counting worksheets: each worksheet has different icons, and students count how many of each icon they see.
  • Students say how many with their talker. They can work at their seats or on the whiteboard.
  • Group 2
  • Snack and break.
  • Students go to the kitchen to help prepare for Friday’s luncheon.
  • Students peel potatoes to prepare for making mashed potatoes, with adults near them to help.
  • Students throw their peels away.
  • Students can cut the potatoes into four once peeled, if they feel comfortable.

Closure

  • Group 1: Students show or tell me one thing they matched or counted today, in any way that works for them.
  • Group 2: We look at the potatoes students prepared and talk about how they will be used for the luncheon on Friday.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students continue helping prepare for Friday’s luncheon and practicing money and counting.
