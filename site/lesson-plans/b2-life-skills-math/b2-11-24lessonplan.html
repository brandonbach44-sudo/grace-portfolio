02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Math and reading skills: Read and use clues, and use numbers 1 to 6, to complete a task.
  • IEP goals: Students follow their individual IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How can clues help me find what I am looking for?
  • How can I use numbers and reading together to complete a task?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Participate in the ELA lesson planned by the ELA teacher.
  • Read and use clues to find turkeys 1 to 6 around the English and math rooms, and match them to their sheet.

Assessment

  • Formative: Student participation in the ELA lesson, as planned by the ELA teacher.
  • Formative: Student scavenger hunt sheet: whether students find turkeys 1 to 6 using the clues.
  • Formative: Observation of how students read and use the clues.

Differentiation

  • Supports: Each student has a sheet with 6 numbered turkeys, so they know what to look for.
  • Process: Clues help students find each turkey, and adults help as needed.
  • Content: The scavenger hunt combines math and reading in one activity.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Tuesday is a half day, so we have the students for 45 minutes.
  • The ELA teacher leads her planned ELA lesson first.
  • Students then move around the English and math rooms to find the turkeys.
  • Adults are nearby to help students read the clues.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students have been reading and using numbers in many of our lessons. The scavenger hunt is a fun way to use both in one activity, and it fits the Thanksgiving week.

Materials Needed

ELA lesson materials (planned by the ELA teacher), scavenger hunt sheets with 6 turkeys labeled 1 to 6, 6 turkeys labeled 1 to 6 hung around the English and math rooms, clues, pencils

Vocabulary

Turkey; clue; number; find; read; scavenger hunt

Lesson Sequence

  • Half Day — 45 minutes
  • We have the students for 45 minutes on Tuesday.
  • ELA Lesson
  • The ELA teacher leads her planned ELA lesson.
  • Turkey Scavenger Hunt
  • Each student gets a sheet with 6 turkeys labeled 1 to 6.
  • The same turkeys, labeled 1 to 6, are set up around the English and math rooms.
  • Students read and use clues to help them find turkeys 1 to 6.
  • This activity uses a little bit of math and reading together.

Closure

  • At the end of class, students share, in any way that works for them, one clue they used to find a turkey.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students return to our usual routine after the holiday.
