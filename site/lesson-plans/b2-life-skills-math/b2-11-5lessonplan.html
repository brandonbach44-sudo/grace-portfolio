02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Functional money skills: Use a bank account to check balances, pay bills, and move money between accounts (making a transfer).
  • IEP goals: Students use their AAC device, their voice, or an adult scribe to communicate, following each student’s IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • What is a transfer, and why do people make one?
  • How do I check how much money is in my checking and savings accounts?
  • How do I know if I have enough money to pay my bills?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Share what they are thankful for in writing, using an AAC device or an adult scribe if needed.
  • Explain, with support, what making a transfer is and why it is important.
  • Log in to Bankability, check their checking and savings account amounts, and pay any bills.
  • Decide whether they need to transfer money from savings to checking to pay their bills.

Assessment

  • Formative: Observation of AAC use or verbal responses during the expectations and the Thankful Thursday warm-up.
  • Formative: Student responses to the recap of making a transfer.
  • Formative: Whether each student checks their accounts and pays their bills in Bankability.
  • Formative: Student response when I ask who needs to make a transfer from savings to checking.

Differentiation

  • AAC: Students use their AAC devices, and an adult writes for the students who need it.
  • Content: The Digitability video gives students pictures and spoken information.
  • Content: We briefly recap the video before students log in to Bankability.
  • Process: Adult support is given as each student needs it.
  • Environment: Students may take a break when they finish.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students begin with the warm-up video and then review the classroom expectations.
  • Students make animal cards for Thankful Thursday, which we hang in the room.
  • I log in to Digitability and play the video for the whole group.
  • Students log in to Bankability on their own devices.
  • Students who finish may take a break.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students already use Bankability and the weekly Digitability videos. Thankful Thursday lets students share something personal before the lesson. The recap connects the video to what students do next, checking their accounts, paying bills, and deciding whether they need a transfer.

Materials Needed

Video about being thankful, classroom expectations, animal-shaped cards, pencils and markers, students’ AAC devices, Digitability “Making a Transfer” video, projector/board, student devices for Bankability

Vocabulary

Thankful; transfer; checking account; savings account; bill; pay; balance

Lesson Sequence

  • Warm-Up Video
  • Students watch a video about being thankful.
  • Expectations
  • We go through the classroom expectations.
  • Thankful Thursday Warm-Up
  • Each student gets a card in the shape of an animal.
  • Students write what they are thankful for, and may use their AAC devices.
  • An adult writes for students who need it.
  • We collect the cards and hang them in the room.
  • Lesson: Making a Transfer
  • I log in to Digitability and play the “Making a Transfer” video.
  • After the video, we briefly recap making a transfer and explain why it is important.
  • Bankability
  • Students log in to Bankability and check how much is in their checking and savings accounts.
  • Students see whether there are any bills to pay, and pay them if there are.
  • I ask if anyone needs to make a transfer from savings to checking to pay their bills.
  • When students finish, they may take a break.

Closure

  • As students finish in Bankability, I ask them to tell me or show me what a transfer is and whether they made one today. Then they may take a break.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students keep using Bankability to check their accounts and pay bills.
