Kitchen Day: Measuring & Mixing

B2_9-23LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 2 Math/Week 4/Lesson Plans

09/23Cooking & Community Skills · Time & MeasurementLife Skills Math (Kitchen/Food Prep)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional life skills, including food preparation and kitchen safety, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Measurement: Measure dry and liquid ingredients using standard units (cups, tablespoons, teaspoons) to follow a recipe.

Essential Questions

  • How do we safely and accurately measure and combine ingredients to follow a recipe?
  • How can visual supports and AAC help us complete a cooking task independently?

Objectives

  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will use picture-based visual instructions and their AAC devices to measure and mix ingredients for their assigned dressing recipe with a partner.
  • Group 2: Students will safely chop vegetables following picture-based instructions with adult/PCA support.
  • Group 2: Students will independently locate their assigned station using the station assignments shown on the projector.
  • All students will wash their hands and follow kitchen safety/hygiene procedures before food preparation.

Assessment

  • Group 1: observation checklist of measuring/mixing steps completed correctly for the assigned dressing
  • Group 1: use of AAC device to request ingredients/tools or indicate next step
  • Group 2: observation of safe vegetable-chopping technique and following of picture instructions
  • Hand-washing completed by all students before starting food prep
  • Group 2: correct station located based on projected assignment

Differentiation

  • Group 1: Symbol Stix visual instructions created to match each student's AAC device vocabulary, with one picture per ingredient and per step
  • Group 1: students paired 2-per-dressing for peer support, with adult/staff support available
  • Group 2: picture-based instructions in the same visual format as Group 1 for consistency, with PCA/adult support at each station
  • Group 2: visible timer displayed for the snack/break period to support predictability and transitions
  • Group 2: station assignments displayed on the projector to reduce transition anxiety and support independent navigation

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual/Symbol Stix supports posted and consistent across both groups
  • Kitchen safety and hygiene routine (hand-washing) reviewed and modeled before food prep begins
  • Station assignments displayed on the projector for Group 2 to reduce confusion during transitions
  • Timer displayed on the board during Group 2's snack/break to support predictable transitions
  • Adults/PCAs paired appropriately with students based on individual support needs
  • All students clean up their workspace before leaving the kitchen, reinforcing kitchen safety and personal responsibility routines.

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • Frame the lesson: today's food prep connects directly to Friday's group lunch, which the students are making themselves — the dressing and vegetables prepared today will be used in the sandwiches.
  • Group 1 (0-5 min): Students enter the kitchen, review the visual schedule, and wash their hands before starting.
  • Group 2 (45-55 min): Students have a 10-minute snack and break, with a timer displayed on the board.

Materials Needed

  • Symbol Stix visual instruction cards for both dressing recipes, matching each student's AAC device vocabulary
  • AAC devices for Group 1 students
  • Measuring cups/spoons, mixing bowls, and mixing utensils for dressing-making
  • Picture-based instructions for vegetable chopping
  • Cutting boards, kid-safe knives, and vegetables for Group 2
  • Visible timer for Group 2's snack/break
  • Projector for displaying Group 2's station assignments
  • Hand-washing supplies

Vocabulary

  • Measure — using a tool (cup, spoon) to find the right amount of an ingredient
  • Mix — combining ingredients together
  • Recipe — a set of steps and ingredients for making food
  • Chop — cutting food into smaller pieces
  • Station — the assigned spot where a student works

Lesson Sequence

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users (0-45 min):
  • (5-10 min) Review station/partner assignments: 2 students per dressing recipe (2 dressings total).
  • (10-35 min) Students measure and mix ingredients for their assigned dressing using the Symbol Stix visual instructions, matching pictures on their AAC devices to request ingredients, tools, or indicate the next step. Adult/staff support provided as needed.
  • (35-40 min) Students clean up their station with support.
  • (40-45 min) Transition: Group 1 exits and Group 2 enters the kitchen.
  • GROUP 2 — Vegetable chopping with PCA/adult support (45-90 min):
  • (55-58 min) Students wash their hands.
  • (58-60 min) Students view their station assignments on the projector and go to their assigned station.
  • (60-85 min) Students chop vegetables following the picture-based instructions, with PCA/adult support at each station.

Closure

(85-90 min) Students clean up their stations. All prepared dressing and chopped vegetables are stored properly for Friday's lunch. Briefly recap what was accomplished and preview that Friday the class will assemble the sandwiches using what was made today.
