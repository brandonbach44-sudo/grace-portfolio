02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Functional money skills: Plan a budget and adjust it when something changes (extra guests need more food).
  • Communication: Read and follow directions, and communicate using an AAC device or with adult help.
  • IEP goals: Students follow their individual IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How can I plan with a budget?
  • What can I do when my plan changes and I need more?
  • How can I read and follow directions in a game?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Work with a budget to plan food for a Friendsgiving, and adjust the plan when three extra guests come.
  • Read a sentence from a sheet, using their AAC device or an adult if needed, and follow directions in Pass the Pumpkin.

Assessment

  • Formative: The News2you Friendsgiving budgeting challenge work, as outlined in that lesson plan.
  • Formative: Observation of whether students read or follow the directions in Pass the Pumpkin, using any communication that works for them.

Differentiation

  • Content: The News2you lesson provides its own supports for the budgeting challenge.
  • AAC: Students who use an AAC device can read the sentence with it, or ask an adult to help them read.
  • Process: The game is active and fun, and every student can take part in some way.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Half day Monday: classes are 45 minutes long.
  • Both block 2 math classes are combined, so there are no Group 1 and Group 2 today.
  • Students work through the budgeting challenge and then play Pass the Pumpkin.
  • Students may use an AAC device or ask an adult for help reading.

Activating Prior Knowledge

The Friendsgiving budgeting challenge gives students a real situation to practice planning with a budget and adjusting when something changes. Pass the Pumpkin is a fun holiday game that gives students a chance to read, listen, and follow directions together.

Materials Needed

News2you Friendsgiving budgeting challenge materials (see the separate News2you lesson plan), pumpkin, Pass the Pumpkin sheet with directions, students’ AAC devices

Vocabulary

Budget; plan; guest; food; cost; pass; read

Lesson Sequence

  • Combined Class — 45 minutes (half day)
  • Both block 2 math classes are together.
  • Friendsgiving Budgeting Challenge
  • Students are given a budget, and the Friendsgiving is all planned.
  • Three extra guests come, and students need to buy more food.
  • This lesson is from News2you, and the details are in a separate lesson plan.
  • Pass the Pumpkin
  • One student starts with a pumpkin and has a sheet with directions on it, such as “Pass the pumpkin to someone wearing red.”
  • The student reads the direction and gives the pumpkin to a student wearing red.
  • The next student reads a sentence and passes the pumpkin.
  • If a student uses an AAC device, they can use it to read, or ask an adult to help them read.

Closure

  • At the end of class, students share, in any way that works for them, one thing they learned about the budget challenge or one direction from Pass the Pumpkin.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students return to our usual routine and keep practicing budgeting and following directions.
