02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Functional money skills: Use a bank account to pay a bill online, make a purchase, and plan a purchase within a budget.
  • CC.2.2.2.A.1: Represent and solve addition problems (adding checking and savings to find a total).
  • IEP goals: Students use their AAC device, their voice, or an adult scribe to communicate, following each student’s IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How do I pay a bill online and make a purchase?
  • How do I find how much money I have in total?
  • How do I choose something to buy that fits my budget?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Share one thing they are thankful for in writing, using an AAC device or an adult scribe if needed.
  • Explain, with support, how to pay a bill online and make a purchase.
  • Log in to Bankability, check for bills, and pay them.
  • Write the amounts in their checking and savings accounts, add them for a total, and choose an item within their budget for tomorrow.

Assessment

  • Formative: Observation of AAC use or verbal responses during the expectations and the thankful activity.
  • Formative: Whether each student logs in to Bankability, checks for bills, and pays them.
  • Formative: Fun Friday worksheet: checking amount, savings amount, total, shopping decision, and an item within budget.

Differentiation

  • Content: The Bankability videos give students pictures and spoken information.
  • AAC: Students use their AAC devices, and an adult writes for students who need it.
  • Process: Adult support is given as each student needs it.
  • Choice: Each student chooses an item that fits their own budget.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • The groups are combined today, so everyone works together for 45 minutes.
  • Students begin with expectations and the ASL sign of the week.
  • A thankful video replaces mindfulness meditation today.
  • Students write one thing they are thankful for on an animal-shaped paper, and we hang them up.
  • Students log in to Bankability on their own devices, with adult help as needed.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students use the daily routine and already use Bankability. The thankful activity lets students share something personal before the lesson. The videos show how to pay a bill and make a purchase, which prepares students to use their accounts and the Fun Friday worksheet for banking tomorrow.

Materials Needed

Classroom expectations, ASL sign of the week, video about being thankful, animal-shaped paper, pencils and markers, students’ AAC devices, Digitability videos on paying a bill online and making a purchase, projector/board, student devices for Bankability, Fun Friday worksheet

Vocabulary

Thankful; pay; bill; online; purchase; checking account; savings account; total; budget

Lesson Sequence

  • Combined Class — 45 minutes
  • Groups 1 and 2 meet together today.
  • Expectations and ASL
  • We go through the classroom expectations.
  • We do the ASL sign of the week.
  • Thankful Video and Activity
  • Students watch a video on being thankful instead of mindfulness meditation.
  • Each student writes one thing they are thankful for on an animal-shaped paper, using their AAC device or an adult scribe if needed.
  • We hang the papers up.
  • Bankability Lesson
  • We watch a video on how to pay a bill online.
  • We watch a video on how to make a purchase.
  • Students log in to their accounts and see if they have bills to pay, and pay them.
  • Fun Friday Worksheet (ready for banking tomorrow)
  • Students write how much is in their checking account and how much is in their savings account.
  • Students add the two amounts to find their total.
  • Students decide whether they will shop.
  • Students choose an item that fits their budget to buy at the store tomorrow.

Closure

  • As students finish their worksheets, I ask them to tell me or show me their total and the item they chose, and how they know it fits their budget.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students use their worksheets to shop at the store tomorrow.
