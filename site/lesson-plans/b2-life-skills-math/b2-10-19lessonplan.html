10/19Money & Banking · Counting, Number Sense & DataLife Skills Math (Money Monday)

PA Standards

  • Functional Math: Count objects on a pictograph and identify the matching number; add prices to find a total cost.
  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional math and consumer skills needed for independent living.
  • Note: verify alignment with individual students' IEP/PASA goals for functional math and AAC communication.

Essential Questions

  • How can we count the pictures on a graph and show the correct number?
  • How do we add up prices to find the total cost of a list of groceries?

Objectives

  • Both groups: Students will participate in a mindfulness meditation, review the class expectations, and learn the ASL sign of the week: 'pay.'
  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will count the number of pictures on a graph by pointing or using their AAC device, then choose the correct number from a list.
  • Group 1: Students will share their response to the class expectations using their AAC device.
  • Group 2: Students will add the prices of the items on their grocery list to find the total cost and write the total on the board.
  • Group 2: Students will complete daily progress monitoring.

Assessment

  • Group 1: accuracy counting the pictures on the graph and choosing the correct number from the list
  • Group 1: participation sharing about the expectations on the AAC device
  • Group 2: accuracy adding the prices on their grocery list and writing the correct total on the board
  • Group 2: progress monitoring data
  • Both groups: participation in meditation, expectations, and the sign of the week

Differentiation

  • Group 1: students may respond by pointing or by using their AAC device
  • Group 1: choosing the answer from a list gives a clear, structured way to show their count
  • Group 1: AAC device used to share thoughts on the class expectations
  • Group 2: each student receives their own individual grocery list matched to their level
  • Visual schedule and consistent warm-up routine maintained for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's routine for each group
  • Calm, quiet environment maintained during the meditation
  • Clear transition between Group 1 and Group 2 blocks
  • Group 1's schedule accounts for leaving early for speech

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users:
  • Warm-up routine: mindfulness meditation; students read the class expectations and share on their AAC device; ASL sign of the week introduced: 'pay.'
  • Group 1 does not do a Money Monday warm-up today and goes right into the pictograph lesson.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Mindfulness meditation video
  • Class expectations
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • ASL sign of the week reference ('pay')
  • Graph with pictures to count, plus a list of answer choices (Group 1)
  • List of grocery items with prices on the board (Group 2)
  • Individual grocery lists for each student (Group 2)
  • Progress monitoring tool/program (Group 2)

Vocabulary

  • Pay — ASL sign of the week
  • Pictograph — a graph that uses pictures/symbols to represent data
  • Count — review
  • Price / total — review
  • Add — review

Lesson Sequence

  • Pictograph lesson, Group 1: students come up to the graph and point or use their AAC device to count the number of pictures. After counting, they choose the correct number from a list. This is all Group 1 has time for, since they leave early for speech.
  • GROUP 2 — no AAC devices:
  • Warm-up routine: mindfulness meditation; students read the class expectations; ASL sign of the week introduced: 'pay.'
  • Money Monday warm-up: a list of grocery items with prices is on the board, and each student receives a list of the items they will be buying. Students add up the prices to find the total cost of their list and write the total on the board.
  • Progress monitoring for Group 2.

Closure

  • Group 1: quick recap of the number counted if time allows before leaving for speech.
  • Group 2: briefly review a few of the totals written on the board and discuss how they were found.
