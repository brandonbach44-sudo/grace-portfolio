Sequencing Numbers & Mean, Median, Mode

B2_10-7LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 2 Math/Week 6/Lesson Plans

10/07Counting, Number Sense & DataLife Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Functional Math: Count and sequence numbers 1-10 and 1-39; read and interpret a pictograph; find the mean, median, and mode of a data set.
  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional math skills needed for independent living and data interpretation.
  • Note: verify alignment with individual students' IEP/PASA goals for functional math and AAC communication.

Essential Questions

  • How do we count and sequence numbers?
  • How can we read a pictograph to understand data?
  • How do we find the mean, median, and mode of a data set, and what do they tell us?

Objectives

  • Group 1: Students will complete a dot-to-dot drawing by counting from 1 to 10 in sequence.
  • Group 1: Students will read a pictograph, count the number of symbols in each row, and write down the total.
  • Group 2: Students will complete a dot-to-dot drawing by sequencing numbers from 1 to 39.
  • Group 2: Students will answer questions about number relationships (e.g., 'What is 5 more than 25?').
  • Group 2: Students will find the mean, median, and mode of a data set shown on a graph.

Assessment

  • Group 1: accuracy completing the 1-10 dot-to-dot drawing
  • Group 1: accuracy counting symbols on the pictograph practice sheet
  • Group 2: accuracy completing the 1-39 dot-to-dot drawing
  • Group 2: accuracy answering number-relationship questions
  • Group 2: accuracy finding mean, median, and mode

Differentiation

  • Group 1: lower number range (1-10) and a concrete, visual counting task matched to their level; AAC device support available
  • Group 2: higher number range (1-39) with the added challenge of number-relationship questions, matched to their higher independence level
  • Teacher models the first mean/median/mode example together with Group 2 before independent practice
  • Visual schedule and consistent routine maintained for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's routine for each group
  • Group 2 has a snack break upon entering, consistent with established routine
  • Clear transition between Group 1 and Group 2 blocks

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users:
  • (warm-up) Dot-to-dot drawing of a car, counting 1 to 10 in sequence.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • Dot-to-dot car worksheet, numbers 1-10 (Group 1)
  • Dot-to-dot city worksheet, numbers 1-39, with number-relationship questions (Group 2)
  • Pictograph practice worksheet (Group 1)
  • Graph/data set worksheet for finding mean, median, and mode (Group 2)

Vocabulary

  • Count / sequence — review
  • Pictograph — a graph that uses pictures/symbols to represent data
  • Mean — the average of a set of numbers
  • Median — the middle number in an ordered set of numbers
  • Mode — the number that appears most often in a set

Lesson Sequence

  • (lesson) Group 1 completes Pictograph Practice: counting the number of an object in each row of a pictograph and writing down the total.
  • GROUP 2 — no AAC devices:
  • (entry) Students have a snack break upon entering.
  • (warm-up) Dot-to-dot drawing of a city, numbers 1-39. Students also answer questions about the numbers (e.g., 'What is 5 more than 25?').
  • (lesson) Group 2 uses a graph to find the mean, median, and mode of a data set, working through the first example together before practicing independently.

Closure

  • Briefly recap what was practiced today: counting and pictographs for Group 1; number relationships and mean/median/mode for Group 2.
  • Collect completed worksheets.
