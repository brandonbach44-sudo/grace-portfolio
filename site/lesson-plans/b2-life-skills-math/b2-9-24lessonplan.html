Kitchen Day: Following a Recipe

09/24Cooking & Community Skills · Time & MeasurementLife Skills Math (Kitchen/Food Prep - Day 2)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional life skills, including food preparation and kitchen safety, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Life Skills: Follow safe food-handling practices (hand-washing, using assigned utensils) when preparing chicken and vegetables.

Essential Questions

  • How do we follow a recipe to safely combine and prepare food?
  • Why is it important to properly wash and prepare vegetables before using them?

Objectives

  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will use picture-based instructions and their AAC devices to combine dressing with chicken according to a recipe, working with a partner, to prepare two separate chicken dishes.
  • Group 2: Students will wash and peel vegetables safely, following picture-based instructions with adult/PCA support.
  • Group 2: Students will independently locate their assigned station using the station assignments shown on the projector.
  • All students will wash their hands and follow kitchen safety/hygiene procedures before and during food preparation.

Assessment

  • Group 1: observation checklist of steps completed correctly for combining dressing and chicken for the assigned dish
  • Group 1: use of AAC device to request ingredients/tools or indicate next step
  • Group 2: observation of safe washing/peeling technique and following of picture instructions
  • Hand-washing completed by all students before starting food prep
  • Group 2: correct station located based on projected assignment

Differentiation

  • Group 1: Symbol Stix visual instructions matching each student's AAC device vocabulary, with one picture per ingredient and per step
  • Group 1: students paired 2-per-dish for peer support, with adult/staff support available
  • Group 2: picture-based instructions in the same visual format as Group 1 for consistency, with PCA/adult support at each station
  • Group 2: visible timer displayed for the snack/break period to support predictability and transitions
  • Group 2: station assignments displayed on the projector to reduce transition anxiety and support independent navigation

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual/Symbol Stix supports posted and consistent across both groups
  • Kitchen safety and hygiene routine (hand-washing) reviewed and modeled before food prep begins
  • Station assignments displayed on the projector for Group 2 to reduce confusion during transitions
  • Timer displayed on the board during Group 2's snack/break to support predictable transitions
  • Adults/PCAs paired appropriately with students based on individual support needs
  • All students clean up their workspace before leaving the kitchen, reinforcing kitchen safety and personal responsibility routines.

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • Frame the lesson: today builds on yesterday's dressing-making, and today's chicken and vegetable prep continues moving the class toward Friday's group lunch.
  • Group 1 (0-5 min): Students enter the kitchen, review the visual schedule, and wash their hands before starting.
  • Group 2 (45-55 min): Students have a 10-minute snack and break, with a timer displayed on the board.

Materials Needed

  • Symbol Stix visual instruction cards for both chicken/dressing dishes, matching each student's AAC device vocabulary
  • AAC devices for Group 1 students
  • Premade dressing, chicken, mixing bowls, and mixing utensils for Group 1
  • Picture-based instructions for washing and peeling vegetables
  • Vegetable peelers, wash bins/colanders, and vegetables for Group 2
  • Visible timer for Group 2's snack/break
  • Projector for displaying Group 2's station assignments
  • Hand-washing supplies

Vocabulary

  • Combine — putting ingredients together to make one dish
  • Recipe — a set of steps and ingredients for making food
  • Peel — removing the outer skin of a vegetable
  • Wash — cleaning food to remove dirt before preparing or eating it
  • Station — the assigned spot where a student works

Lesson Sequence

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users (0-45 min):
  • (5-10 min) Review station/partner assignments: 2 students per dish (2 chicken dishes total).
  • (10-35 min) Students combine the premade dressing with chicken using the Symbol Stix visual instructions, matching pictures on their AAC devices to request ingredients, tools, or indicate the next step. Adult/staff support provided as needed.
  • (35-40 min) Students clean up their station with support.
  • (40-45 min) Transition: Group 1 exits and Group 2 enters the kitchen.
  • GROUP 2 — Washing and peeling vegetables with PCA/adult support (45-90 min):
  • (55-58 min) Students wash their hands.
  • (58-60 min) Students view their station assignments on the projector and go to their assigned station.
  • (60-85 min) Students wash and peel their assigned vegetables following the picture-based instructions, with PCA/adult support at each station.

Closure

(85-90 min) Students clean up their stations. All prepared chicken dishes and washed/peeled vegetables are stored properly for Friday's lunch. Briefly recap what was accomplished and preview that the vegetables will be chopped in an upcoming lesson before assembly on Friday.
