10/08Money & BankingLife Skills Math (Bankability Prep)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Analyze consumer and personal finance skills, including tracking account balances, bills, and budgeting for purchases, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Math: Add savings and checking account balances to determine total available funds; compare prices to available funds to make spending/saving decisions.

Essential Questions

  • How do we keep track of money in a savings and checking account?
  • How can we decide whether to spend or save our money?

Objectives

  • Students will log into Digitability and identify the balances in their savings and checking accounts.
  • Students will add their savings and checking balances to determine their total available money.
  • Students will identify whether they have any bills to pay and the amount owed.
  • Students will decide whether they want to shop at tomorrow's Bankability store, based on their available funds.
  • Students will compare prize prices to their available funds to decide if they can afford an item or need to save for something more expensive.
  • Students will learn the ASL sign of the week: 'deposit.'

Assessment

  • Completed 'Fun Friday' Bankability sheet (account balances, total, bills, shopping decision)
  • Accuracy adding savings and checking account balances
  • Participation in the ASL sign of the week
  • Participation in the Digitability lesson

Differentiation

  • Group 1 (AAC users): Digitability walkthrough and Fun Friday sheet supported with visuals/AAC device use
  • Group 2: more independent login and navigation of Digitability, with more complex budgeting reasoning (e.g., saving for a more expensive item)
  • Teacher models the Fun Friday sheet process step-by-step before independent work
  • Visual schedule and consistent routine maintained for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's routine for each group
  • Group 2 has a snack break upon entering, consistent with established routine
  • Clear transition between Group 1 and Group 2 blocks
  • Consistent weekly routine of reviewing an ASL sign of the week

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users:
  • (entry) Students enter, gather materials, and complete their usual warm-up routine.
  • (sign of the week) Introduce and demonstrate the ASL sign for 'deposit.'

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • Devices with Digitability access
  • 'Fun Friday' Bankability worksheet
  • ASL sign of the week reference ('deposit')
  • List of Bankability store prize prices

Vocabulary

  • Deposit — ASL sign of the week; adding money into an account
  • Savings account / checking account — review
  • Bill — an amount of money owed
  • Balance / total — review

Lesson Sequence

  • (digitability lesson) Short lesson on logging into and navigating Digitability.
  • (fun friday sheet) Students log into Digitability, check their savings and checking account balances, add the two together for a total, check for any bills and record the amount owed, and decide whether they want to go shopping tomorrow at the Bankability store. Students may look at the prize prices listed to decide what they can afford or whether to save for something more expensive.
  • GROUP 2 — no AAC devices:
  • (entry) Students have a snack break upon entering.
  • (warm-up) Students complete their usual warm-up routine.
  • (sign of the week) Introduce and demonstrate the ASL sign for 'deposit.'
  • (digitability lesson) Short lesson on logging into and navigating Digitability, with more independent navigation expected.
  • (fun friday sheet) Students independently log into Digitability, check their account balances, add them for a total, check for bills, and decide whether to shop at tomorrow's Bankability store, comparing prize prices to their available funds.

Closure

  • Briefly recap: how much money do you have, and what did you decide about shopping tomorrow?
  • Preview that tomorrow's Bankability store will use the decisions made on today's Fun Friday sheet.
