02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Functional skills: Follow multistep directions to complete a craft project.
  • Social skills: Work cooperatively with a peer buddy and communicate using an AAC device or voice.

Essential Questions

  • How can I follow directions to make something with a buddy?
  • How can I work well with a classmate?
  • How can my work help our school community?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Follow instructions with a general education buddy to make paper flowers for the Día de los Muertos bulletin board.
  • Communicate with their buddy using their AAC device or voice.
  • Color a Día de los Muertos picture after their break.

Assessment

  • Formative: Observation of whether students follow the steps with their buddy.
  • Formative: Observation of communication with buddies, using AAC devices or voice.
  • Product: The finished paper flowers for the hallway bulletin board.
  • Product: The finished coloring picture.

Differentiation

  • Peer support: Each student works with a general education buddy who has the instructions and works with them.
  • Process: Adults give help as each student needs it.
  • AAC: Students use their AAC devices or voice to communicate with their buddy.
  • Environment: When finished, students take a snack and leisure break, then choose to color.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Groups 1 and 2 are combined today instead of split.
  • Students work in pairs with a general education buddy from a Spanish class.
  • Buddies have the instructions and work with our students to make paper flowers.
  • Adults circulate to support students and buddies.
  • After finishing, students take a snack and leisure break and then color Día de los Muertos pictures.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students already follow multistep directions in our classroom routines. Working with a buddy on a hands-on project gives students a chance to share what they can do with classmates. The flowers will be displayed on a hallway bulletin board for the whole school to see.

Materials Needed

Paper flower instructions and materials, students’ AAC devices, snack and leisure items, Día de los Muertos coloring pictures, coloring supplies

Vocabulary

Día de los Muertos; flower; paper; buddy; directions; color; break

Lesson Sequence

  • Combined Class
  • Groups 1 and 2 meet together today.
  • Paper Flowers with Buddies
  • General education Spanish students arrive. Each is paired with a buddy from our class.
  • Buddies have the instructions and work with our students to make paper flowers for the Día de los Muertos bulletin board in the hallway.
  • Adults circulate and help as needed, and students may use their AAC devices to communicate.
  • Snack and Leisure Break
  • When students finish their flowers, they take a snack and leisure break.
  • Coloring
  • Students color Día de los Muertos coloring pictures.

Closure

  • At the end of class, students share one thing they made or colored, using their AAC device or voice. The flowers will go on the hallway bulletin board.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: We return to our usual two-group routine.
