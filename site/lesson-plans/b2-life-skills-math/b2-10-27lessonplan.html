Cooking with Symbol Directions

10/27Cooking & Community Skills · Time & MeasurementLife Skills Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.4.3.A.2: Solve problems involving measurement of liquid volumes (measuring amounts in cups).
  • Functional skills: Follow multistep directions to complete a functional cooking task, using symbol-supported directions.
  • IEP goals: Students use their AAC device or their voice to communicate, following each student’s IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How can pictures and symbols help me follow directions?
  • How do measurements help me make a recipe the right way?
  • How can I work safely and help a friend in the kitchen?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Group 1: Follow printed Symbol Stix directions to add chicken and seasoning to the crockpot and start boiling water for noodles, and boil the noodles if time allows.
  • Group 2: Use measurements in cups to make the rest of the mac and cheese.
  • Both groups: Work with an adult in the kitchen, take turns, and use their AAC device or voice to communicate.

Assessment

  • Group 1: Observation of whether students follow each Symbol Stix direction and complete each step.
  • Group 2: Observation of whether students measure the correct amount with the measuring cups.
  • Both groups: Notes on AAC use or verbal communication during the cooking task.
  • Both groups: The finished food (chicken in the crockpot, noodles, and mac and cheese) shows whether the steps were followed.

Differentiation

  • Group 1: Printed directions use Symbol Stix images that match the symbols on students’ talkers.
  • Group 1: I model my AAC device while giving directions.
  • Group 2: Measurements are similar to the Measurement Monday warm-up, so students practice a skill they have seen.
  • Both groups: Adult support is given as each student needs it.
  • Reinforcement: Fake money is earned for participating and helping a friend.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students follow the same classroom routines and expectations they already know, plus the kitchen safety rules reviewed before cooking.
  • Group 1 has printed Symbol Stix directions to follow, one step at a time.
  • Adults supervise all use of the stove and crockpot.
  • Group 2 takes a snack/leisure break first, then starts cooking.
  • Students earn fake money for participating and helping a friend.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Yesterday’s Measurement Monday warm-up gave students practice measuring amounts in cups, and today they use those measurements to cook. The Symbol Stix directions use images students already know from their talkers. Cooking a real meal motivates students and connects math to everyday life.

Materials Needed

Printed Symbol Stix directions, chicken, seasoning, crockpot, pot, stove, water, noodles, mac and cheese ingredients, measuring cups, spoons, bowls, students’ AAC devices, teacher AAC device, fake money, timer, snack and leisure items

Vocabulary

Cup; measure; amount; boil; stir; chicken; seasoning; noodles; mac and cheese

Lesson Sequence

  • Group 1 — cooking
  • Students use the printed Symbol Stix directions, which match the symbols on their talkers.
  • Students put the chicken and seasoning in the crockpot, following the directions.
  • Students put water on the stove to boil for the noodles.
  • If there is time, students boil the noodles.
  • I model my AAC device while giving directions and give help as each student needs it.
  • I hand out fake money for participating and helping a friend.
  • Group 2 — begins at 9:52
  • Students grab their materials.
  • Snack and leisure break until 10:08 (timer on the board).
  • Students make the rest of the mac and cheese using measurements, similar to the Measurement Monday warm-up.
  • Students measure the amounts in cups, with adult support as needed.
  • I hand out fake money for participating and helping a friend.

Closure

  • Group 1: Students and I look at the finished steps on the Symbol Stix directions. I use my AAC device to ask students to share one thing they did.
  • Group 2: I ask students to say or show one measurement they used and one thing they did to help make the mac and cheese.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students continue practicing measurements and following directions, and use their fake money to shop at the end of the week.
