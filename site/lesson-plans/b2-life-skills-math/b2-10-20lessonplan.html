10/20Time & MeasurementLife Skills Math (Time Tuesday)

PA Standards

  • Functional Math: Tell time on an analog clock and match a written time to the correct clock.
  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional life skills, including telling time, needed for independent living.
  • Note: verify alignment with individual students' IEP/PASA goals for functional math and AAC communication.

Essential Questions

  • How do we read a clock and show a given time?
  • How do we match a written time to the correct clock?

Objectives

  • Both groups: Students will participate in a mindfulness meditation, review the class expectations, and learn the ASL sign of the week.
  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will set their clock to match the time shown on the board and on the teacher's clock.
  • Group 1: Students will complete daily progress monitoring.
  • Group 2: Students will match a list of written times to the correct clocks.
  • Group 2: Students will practice foundational math skills independently via IXL.

Assessment

  • Group 1: accuracy setting the clock to match the time on the board and the teacher's clock
  • Group 1: progress monitoring data
  • Group 2: accuracy matching written times to the correct clocks
  • Group 2: IXL performance data
  • Both groups: participation in meditation, expectations, and the sign of the week

Differentiation

  • Group 1: the teacher models the time on her own clock so students have a visual to copy onto their clocks
  • Group 1: the time is displayed on the board as well, giving a second visual reference
  • Group 1: AAC device used to share thoughts on the class expectations
  • Group 2: matching task gives a structured, concrete way to show understanding of time
  • Group 2: IXL provides practice at each student's individual level
  • Visual schedule and consistent warm-up routine maintained for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's routine for each group
  • Calm, quiet environment maintained during the meditation
  • Clear transition between Group 1 and Group 2 blocks
  • Consistent daily warm-up routine for both groups

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users:
  • Warm-up routine: mindfulness meditation; students read the class expectations and share on their AAC device; ASL sign of the week introduced.
  • Time Tuesday warm-up: a time is displayed on the board and the teacher sets her clock to that time. Students show the same time on their own clocks.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • Mindfulness meditation video
  • Class expectations
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • ASL sign of the week reference
  • Time displayed on the board, teacher's demonstration clock, and individual student clocks (Group 1)
  • List of times and clocks for matching (Group 2)
  • Progress monitoring tool/program (Group 1)
  • Devices for IXL (Group 2)

Vocabulary

  • Clock — review
  • Hour / minute — review
  • Match — pairing a written time with the clock that shows it
  • ASL sign of the week

Lesson Sequence

  • Progress monitoring for Group 1.
  • GROUP 2 — no AAC devices:
  • Warm-up routine: mindfulness meditation; students read the class expectations; ASL sign of the week introduced.
  • Time Tuesday warm-up: students are given a list of times and a set of clocks and match each time to the correct clock.
  • IXL practice for Group 2.

Closure

  • Briefly recap what was practiced today: showing and matching times on a clock.
  • Preview that next Tuesday's Time Tuesday warm-up will continue building on these time-telling skills.
