02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Math skills: Count and sort objects by color to complete a counting task.
  • Math skills: Complete and extend patterns, and count items.
  • Literacy skills: Match words and pictures, including compound words, and listen to descriptions to find the correct word.
  • IEP goals: Students follow their individual IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How can counting and sorting help me finish a task?
  • How can I find and continue a pattern?
  • How can pictures and descriptions help me find the right word?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Math: Count and sort cotton puffs by color to stuff a turkey cup, and complete fall patterns, a fall I Spy counting activity, and a fall counting worksheet.
  • Literacy: Match compound words and their pictures, complete a compound word worksheet and a turkey puzzle, and match words and pictures in bingo by listening to descriptions.
  • Work in a small group at each station for 15 minutes.

Assessment

  • Stuff the Turkey: The finished turkey cup, with the correct number of each color of puff.
  • Fall math: The completed fall patterns, I Spy count, and counting worksheet.
  • Compound words: Matched compound word cards and pictures, the compound word worksheet, and the turkey puzzle.
  • Bingo: Student responses to the descriptions and correct word matches on their bingo card.
  • All stations: Observation by the teacher at each station.

Differentiation

  • Grouping: Small groups of 4 or 5 students at each station.
  • Support: Every student has a PCA or adult to help at the fall math station.
  • Content: Hands-on activities, pictures, and descriptions give students more than one way to show what they know.
  • Support: The counting sheet tells students how many of each color to use.
  • Choice: Students may color in the pictures on the compound word worksheet if they would like.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Class is different today. There are 4 Math and Literacy stations, led by 4 different teachers in 4 different classrooms.
  • Students work in groups of 4 or 5, and spend 15 minutes at each station.
  • Two stations are math related and two are ELA related.
  • A PCA or adult helps each student at the fall math station.
  • Students earn a prize in bingo for calling bingo when they have it.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students use hands-on fall activities to practice counting, sorting, patterns, and matching words and pictures. Working in small groups with different teachers gives them a fun change from our usual routine and a chance to practice skills in new ways.

Materials Needed

Stuff the Turkey: turkey template, crayons/markers, scissors, paper cups, glue dots, counting sheet, colorful cotton puffs. Fall math: fall patterns, fall I Spy, fall counting worksheet. Compound words: compound word cards and picture cards, compound word worksheet, coloring supplies, turkey puzzle. Bingo: bingo cards (word and picture), descriptions and words to read, bingo chips, prizes

Vocabulary

Count; sort; color; pattern; compound word; match; description; bingo; turkey

Lesson Sequence

  • Math and Literacy Stations — 4 stations, 15 minutes each
  • Students are in groups of 4 or 5 and rotate through all 4 stations, each led by a different teacher in a different classroom.
  • Station 1 (Math): Stuff the Turkey
  • Students color a turkey template (tail feathers and a head with a face).
  • Students cut out the template and stick the pieces onto a paper cup with glue dots.
  • Students get a counting sheet and a handful of colorful cotton puffs.
  • The counting sheet tells students how many of each color to put in the turkey cup. Students count and sort the puffs by color to stuff their turkey.
  • Station 2 (Math): Fall Math
  • Students complete fall patterns.
  • Students do a fall I Spy and count the number of items they see.
  • Students complete a fall counting worksheet.
  • Every student has a PCA or adult to help.
  • Station 3 (ELA): Compound Words
  • Students match the compound word cards, and then find the pictures that match each compound word.
  • Students complete a compound word worksheet, writing compound words based on the pictures. They may color in the pictures if they would like.
  • Students can also do a puzzle that makes a turkey, which is a way to match shapes together and create a picture.
  • Station 4 (ELA): Bingo
  • Each student gets a bingo card with words and pictures.
  • The teacher reads a description of a word and then reads the word, so students hear the description.
  • Students look on their card to see if they have the word and picture, and cover it with a bingo chip if they do.
  • Students call bingo to receive a prize if they have it.

Closure

  • As students finish the last station, I ask them to share one thing they made or did at the stations. Students bring their finished work back with them.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students return to our usual routine and keep practicing counting, patterns, and word matching.
