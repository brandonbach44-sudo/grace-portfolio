02/11Life Skills Math

PA Standards

  • Functional money skills: Understand that a transfer moves money between a savings account and a checking account, and why people make transfers.
  • IEP goals: Students communicate in any way that works for them, following each student’s IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • What is a transfer, and why do people make one?
  • How can I move money between my savings and checking accounts?
  • How can I get ready for banking tomorrow?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Practice math skills on their IXL recommendations.
  • Explain, with support, what a transfer is and why people transfer money between savings and checking accounts.
  • Fill out their Fun Friday sheet to get ready for Bankability the next day.

Assessment

  • Practice data: IXL recommendations during the 10-minute warm-up.
  • Formative: Student responses during the discussion about making a transfer.
  • Formative: Fun Friday sheet.

Differentiation

  • Supports: The timer shows students when the snack and break end.
  • Content: The video on making a transfer gives students pictures and spoken information.
  • Process: We talk through making a transfer together before students fill out their sheet.
  • Process: IXL recommendations are personalized to each student.
  • Process: Adult support is given as each student needs it.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Both groups are combined today, from 9:52 to 10:42.
  • Students begin with a snack and break until 10:08, with a timer on the board.
  • When the timer goes off, students get their materials.
  • Students follow the usual routine: mindfulness meditation, expectations, and the ASL sign of the week.
  • Students fill out the Fun Friday sheet to get ready for Bankability the next day.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students use the daily routine and already use Bankability. Learning about making a transfer helps them understand why we move money between accounts, and filling out the Fun Friday sheet gets them ready for banking the next day.

Materials Needed

Timer, snack items, mindfulness meditation, classroom expectations, ASL sign of the week (“cheap”), student devices for IXL, Digitability video on making a transfer, projector/board, Fun Friday sheet, pencils

Vocabulary

Cheap; transfer; savings account; checking account; account; bank

Lesson Sequence

  • Combined Class — 9:52–10:42
  • Snack and Break — until 10:08
  • Students come in and have snack and break.
  • The timer goes off, and students get their materials.
  • Mindfulness, Expectations, and ASL
  • Mindfulness meditation.
  • We go through the classroom expectations.
  • ASL sign of the week, “cheap.”
  • Warm-Up: IXL — 10 minutes
  • Students work on their IXL recommendations.
  • Bankability Lesson
  • I show a video on making a transfer.
  • We discuss making a transfer from savings to checking accounts and from checking to savings accounts, and why we do it.
  • Fun Friday Sheet
  • Students fill out their Fun Friday sheet to get ready for Bankability the next day.

Closure

  • As students finish their sheets, I ask them to tell me or show me what a transfer is and why we make one, in any way that works for them.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students use Bankability the next day and use their Fun Friday sheets.
