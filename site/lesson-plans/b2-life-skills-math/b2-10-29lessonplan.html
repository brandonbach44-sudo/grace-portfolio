Paycheck Day: Word Problems & Spending Choices

B2_10-29LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 2 Math/Week 9/Lesson Plans

10/29Money & Banking · Counting, Number Sense & DataLife Skills Math

PA Standards

  • CC.2.2.2.A.1: Represent and solve addition problems.
  • CC.2.2.3.A.1: Represent and solve problems involving multiplication.
  • Money skills: Functional skills: use money and a bank account to plan a purchase and check that there is enough money.
  • IEP goals: Students use their AAC device or their voice to communicate, following each student’s IEP goals.

Essential Questions

  • How can a calculator help me solve a word problem?
  • How do I know if I have enough money to buy what I want?
  • How can I make a good choice about spending my money?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Group 1: Count the pictures in a word problem and use a calculator to add the objects together.
  • Group 2: Solve multiplication word problems using a calculator.
  • Both groups: Watch a video about receiving a paycheck, check and write down the amount in their bank account, decide whether to shop at Bankability, and choose items they can afford using the prices next to the pictures.

Assessment

  • Group 1: Student answers to the picture word problem and use of the calculator to add.
  • Group 2: Student answers to the multiplication word problems.
  • Both groups: The amount each student writes down from their bank account.
  • Both groups: Items each student chooses, checked against the amount in their account, show whether they know they have enough.
  • Both groups: Observation of AAC use or verbal responses during the daily routine.

Differentiation

  • Group 1: The word problem uses pictures to count and a calculator to add.
  • Group 1: I model my AAC device while asking questions.
  • Group 2: Multiplication word problems with a calculator.
  • Both groups: Choices are based on pictures with prices next to them, so students can pick items they want.
  • Both groups: Students decide for themselves whether to shop the next day.
  • Both groups: Adult support is given as each student needs it.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Same daily routine every day: materials, mindfulness, expectations, and ASL sign of the week.
  • Think About It Thursday is a familiar weekly routine.
  • Each student has a folder with pictures and prices.
  • Group 2 takes a snack/leisure break first, with a timer on the board.
  • Adults give help as needed while students check their accounts and make choices.

Activating Prior Knowledge

The daily routines and the weekly Think About It Thursday are familiar to students. The warm-up reviews counting, adding, and multiplying with a calculator, which students use to make money decisions. Shopping at Bankability the next day motivates students and connects math to real life.

Materials Needed

Visual schedule, projector/board, mindfulness video, students’ AAC devices, teacher AAC device, classroom expectations, ASL sign of the week, Think About It Thursday word problems, calculators, Digitability paycheck video, students’ bank account information, student folders with pictures and prices, pencils, timer

Vocabulary

Count; add; total; multiply; paycheck; bank account; amount; price; enough; shop

Lesson Sequence

  • Group 1 — 9:15–9:52
  • Students gather their materials.
  • Daily mindfulness.
  • Review the expectations: I use my AAC device to find them and students use theirs.
  • ASL sign of the week.
  • Think About It Thursday: students count the pictures in a word problem and use a calculator to add the objects together.
  • Bankability prep: students watch the Digitability video about receiving a paycheck.
  • Students check their bank account and write down the amount.
  • Students decide whether they want to shop at Bankability the next day.
  • Students choose what they want using the pictures and prices in their folders, making sure they have enough.
  • I model my AAC device throughout.
  • Group 2 — begins at 9:52
  • Students grab their materials.
  • Snack and leisure break until 10:08 (timer on the board).
  • Daily meditation.
  • Review the expectations aloud.
  • ASL sign of the week.
  • Think About It Thursday: students solve multiplication word problems using a calculator.
  • Bankability prep: students watch the Digitability video about receiving a paycheck.
  • Students check their bank account and write down the amount.
  • Students decide whether they want to shop at Bankability the next day.
  • Students choose what they want using the pictures and prices in their folders, making sure they have enough.

Closure

  • Group 1: I use my AAC device to ask students to share one thing they chose and whether they have enough money for it.
  • Group 2: I ask students to tell me the amount in their account, what they chose, and how they know they have enough.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students who decide to shop go to Bankability the next day and use their choices from today.
