Time Tuesday: Clocks, Pictographs & Payroll

09/29Time & Measurement · Money & BankingLife Skills Math (Time Tuesday)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Apply functional life skills, including telling time and tracking work hours/pay, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Math: Tell time to the hour and half hour; read and interpret a pictograph; add hours worked and calculate pay.

Essential Questions

  • How do we tell time to the hour and to the half hour?
  • How can reading a pictograph help us understand information?
  • How do employees track hours worked and calculate pay?

Objectives

  • Group 1 (AAC users): Students will set a manipulative clock to match a time shown to the hour and communicate the time using their AAC device.
  • Group 1: Students will complete News2You Pictograph Practice, counting symbols on a pictograph to interpret data.
  • Group 2: Students will tell time to the half hour using the News2You warm-up.
  • Group 2: Students will complete IXL practice.
  • Group 2 (Payroll job): One student will review classmates' time cards from the past few weeks, add up shifts worked, and calculate pay owed.
  • Both groups: Students will learn the math sign of the week ('cost') and participate in mindfulness and expectations review.

Assessment

  • Group 1: accuracy of clock matched to the stated hour and AAC device response stating the time
  • Group 1: accuracy counting symbols during Pictograph Practice
  • Group 2: accuracy telling time to the half hour during the News2You warm-up
  • Group 2: IXL performance data
  • Payroll student: accuracy of shift totals and pay calculations
  • Participation in math sign of the week, mindfulness, and expectations review

Differentiation

  • Group 1: manipulative clock paired with AAC device modeling; times kept to the hour for lower complexity
  • Group 1: pictograph symbols kept simple and consistent to support accurate counting
  • Group 2: half-hour time-telling and IXL matched to their higher independence level
  • Payroll job differentiated as a real-world vocational task for the student ready for that level of responsibility
  • Math sign of the week and mindfulness routine kept consistent across both groups for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual routine and materials list posted on the board
  • Clear transition between Group 1 and Group 2 blocks
  • Payroll job station set up separately to support the student's focus while reviewing time cards
  • Consistent daily elements (math sign of the week, mindfulness, expectations) maintained across both groups

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • GROUP 1 — AAC users:
  • (warm-up) Clock warm-up to the hour: teacher states and shows a time on their clock; students set their own clock to match and then tell the teacher the time using their AAC device.

Materials Needed

  • Visual routine and materials list (board/projector)
  • AAC devices (Group 1)
  • Teacher demonstration clock and individual student clocks (Group 1)
  • News2You telling-time-to-the-half-hour warm-up (Group 2)
  • Math sign of the week materials ("cost")
  • Mindfulness meditation video
  • News2You Pictograph Practice materials (Group 1)
  • IXL access (Group 2)
  • Classmates' time cards from the past few weeks (for the Payroll job)
  • Payroll calculation sheet/tool

Vocabulary

  • Hour / half hour — units of telling time
  • Pictograph — a graph that uses pictures/symbols to represent data
  • Cost — math sign of the week
  • Shift — a period of time an employee works
  • Payroll — the process of calculating how much employees are paid

Lesson Sequence

  • (math sign of the week) Introduce and demonstrate the sign for 'cost.'
  • (mindfulness) Mindfulness meditation video.
  • (expectations) Review class expectations.
  • (activity) Group 1 completes News2You Pictograph Practice: students look at graphs with pictures and count the symbols to interpret the data.
  • GROUP 2:
  • (warm-up) News2You telling time to the half hour.
  • (math sign of the week) Introduce and demonstrate the sign for 'cost.'
  • (mindfulness) Mindfulness meditation video.
  • (expectations) Review class expectations.
  • (activity) Most students complete IXL practice. One student is assigned the 'Payroll' job: reviewing classmates' time cards from the past few weeks, adding up how many shifts each classmate worked, and calculating how much they should be paid.

Closure

  • Briefly recap: what time did you practice telling today? What did you learn from the pictograph or from IXL?
  • For the Payroll student: briefly review their totals and calculations.
  • Preview that next Tuesday's Time Tuesday warm-up will continue building on these time-telling skills.
