Greater Than, Less Than & Understanding Income

B2_10-21LessonPlan.docx

Also in: Block 2 Math/Week 8/Lesson Plans

10/21Counting, Number Sense & Data · Money & BankingLife Skills Math (Bankability Prep)

PA Standards

  • PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Analyze consumer and personal finance skills, including income, account balances, and deciding what is affordable, needed for independent living.
  • Functional Math: Compare numbers using greater than and less than; compare prices to available funds.
  • Note: verify alignment with individual students' IEP/PASA goals for functional math.

Essential Questions

  • How do we tell which number is greater than or less than another?
  • What is income, and where does it come from?
  • How do we decide what we can afford to buy?

Objectives

  • Students will identify which number is greater than or less than another during the 'What's the Number Wednesday' warm-up.
  • Students will explain that income is money earned, and connect it to getting paid at their work ed jobs.
  • Students will log into their Bankability accounts and record how much money they have.
  • Students will decide whether they will shop on Friday (yes/no).
  • Students will compare the prices on the item list to their available money and circle what they can afford and want to buy.

Assessment

  • Accuracy identifying greater than/less than numbers during the warm-up
  • Participation in the income video discussion
  • Completed Fun Friday form: account amount recorded, yes/no shopping choice marked, items circled
  • Whether the items circled fit within the amount of money the student has

Differentiation

  • Video from the Bankability website presents income visually and in simple terms
  • Income is connected to students' own experience: getting paid at their work ed jobs
  • Fun Friday form and item list provide a structured, concrete way to make a spending decision
  • Adult support available for logging in, reading the account amount, and comparing it to prices
  • Visual schedule and consistent morning routine maintained for predictability

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's activities
  • Usual morning routine maintained
  • Clear verbal and visual cues signal transitions between the warm-up, video, and Fun Friday form
  • Students reminded that there is no class Thursday, which is why the Friday form is completed today

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • Usual morning routine.
  • Warm-up: 'What's the Number Wednesday.' Numbers are shown on the board, and students tell which number is greater than or less than the other.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule (board/projector)
  • 'What's the Number Wednesday' numbers on the board
  • Video on income from the Bankability website
  • Devices with Bankability access
  • Fun Friday form for Bankability
  • List of items students can buy, with prices
  • Pencils

Vocabulary

  • Income — money you earn, such as pay from a job
  • Greater than / less than — review
  • Balance — the amount of money in an account (review)
  • Afford — having enough money to buy something (review)

Lesson Sequence

  • Bankability lesson: watch the video from the website about income. Discuss what income is. Explain that when students go to work (they are in work ed), they get paid, and that pay is their income.
  • Fun Friday form: because there is no class Thursday, students fill out their Friday Bankability form today. Students go into their Bankability accounts and write down how much money they have, then choose yes or no for whether they will be shopping on Friday.
  • Students look at the list of items they can buy, decide whether they have enough money, and circle what they want to buy.

Closure

  • Briefly recap: what is income, and are you shopping on Friday?
  • Remind students there is no class Thursday and that the Bankability store is Friday.
