Perseverance

10/05Social & Emotional SkillsEssential Skills and Strategies (ESS)

PA Standards

PA SEL Competency — Self-Management: Demonstrate perseverance in the face of challenges and use effective coping strategies to manage stress and reach goals.

Essential Questions

  • What does it mean to persevere when something gets hard?
  • What coping strategies can help us keep going instead of giving up?

Objectives

  • Students will participate in a mindfulness video as a calming strategy.
  • Students will collaborate to solve a hands-on challenge (lifting a cup without touching it) as an introduction to perseverance.
  • Students will read about perseverance and identify a time they quit something before finishing.
  • Students will brainstorm personal strategies for persevering when things get difficult.
  • Students will identify why using coping strategies is important when faced with challenges.

Assessment

  • Participation in the mindfulness video
  • Participation and collaboration during the cup-lifting challenge
  • Completed perseverance worksheet (strategy definitions, personal example, brainstormed 5th strategy)
  • Participation in the closing discussion

Differentiation

  • Visual schedule posted for predictability
  • Cup-lifting challenge gives a hands-on, concrete experience of perseverance rather than only discussing it abstractly
  • Worksheet includes guided prompts to scaffold personal reflection
  • Option to share a personal example aloud or keep it private/written only
  • Visual summary slide reinforces key vocabulary and strategies at the end of the lesson

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's activities
  • Calm, quiet environment maintained during the mindfulness video
  • Collaborative, low-pressure framing for the cup-lifting challenge — multiple attempts are expected and are part of the point
  • Positive reinforcement for perseverance shown during the activity itself

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • (0-5 min) Students enter and complete their daily check-in.
  • (5-15 min) Warm-up activity of choice.
  • (15-20 min) Mindfulness video.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule
  • Daily check-in sheet or digital tool
  • Warm-up activity choices
  • Mindfulness video
  • Cup, string, and rubber band for the perseverance challenge (one set per small group)
  • Perseverance worksheet (printed copies)
  • Closing summary slide

Vocabulary

  • Perseverance — to keep going on a task even when it becomes difficult, without giving up
  • Coping strategy — a tool used to manage a difficult feeling or situation
  • Motivation — the reason behind why we want to do something

Lesson Sequence

  • (20-35 min) Group activity: using only string tied to rubber bands (no hands touching the cup directly), small groups work together to lift a cup off the table and move it, introduced as a hands-on first experience of perseverance — it will likely take several tries, and that's the point.
  • (35-45 min) Transition discussion: connect the challenge to perseverance — ask students what made the activity hard, whether they wanted to give up, and what helped them keep trying.
  • (45-75 min) Perseverance worksheet: students read 'What is Perseverance?', reflect on a time they quit something before finishing, and brainstorm strategies for persevering (including writing their own 5th strategy).
  • (75-85 min) Closing summary slide: review that things will get hard sometimes, and that having coping strategies ready before that happens helps us respond instead of giving up. Tie together the mindfulness video, the cup challenge, and the worksheet strategies as three examples of tools practiced today.

Closure

  • (85-90 min) Briefly recap the day's three activities and how each connects to perseverance and coping strategies.
  • Collect the perseverance worksheet.
