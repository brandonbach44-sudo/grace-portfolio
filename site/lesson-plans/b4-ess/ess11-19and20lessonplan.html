11/19Essential Skills and Strategies

PA Standards

  • Skills: Self-awareness and emotional regulation: identify emotions and how we react to them.
  • Skills: Self-advocacy and communication: write a clear email to a teacher using a template.

Essential Questions

  • How do my emotions affect the way I react?
  • How can I use strategies to manage my emotions?
  • How can I communicate clearly with my teachers?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Complete their daily check-in on Canvas.
  • Write their student-led communication email using the template they were given.
  • Connect the emotions and reactions in Inside Out 2 to what we have learned about emotional regulation.

Assessment

  • Formative: Daily check-in on Canvas.
  • Formative: Student-led communication email, written using the template.
  • Formative: Observation of student participation and comments while watching Inside Out 2.

Differentiation

  • Supports: Students use a template for their email, so they know what to include.
  • Process: Students work at their own pace on the check-in and the email.
  • Content: The movie uses pictures and characters to show emotions, which helps students connect to what we have discussed.
  • Environment: The movie is also an entertaining break for students.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Students begin with the daily check-in on Canvas, which is a familiar routine.
  • Students complete their email independently using the template they were given.
  • After students finish, we watch Inside Out 2 together.
  • If we do not finish the movie, we finish it next class.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students have been learning about emotional regulation and how we react when we feel certain emotions. Inside Out 2 is a good way to sum up what we have talked about, and it gives students an entertaining break after they finish their work.

Materials Needed

Computers for Canvas, daily check-in, student-led communication email template, Inside Out 2, projector/screen

Vocabulary

Emotion; emotional regulation; reaction; communication; self-advocacy; email; template

Lesson Sequence

  • Daily Check-In
  • Students complete their daily check-in on Canvas.
  • Student-Led Communication Email
  • Students complete their student-led communication email using the template they have been given.
  • Inside Out 2
  • When students finish, we watch Inside Out 2.
  • We have learned a lot about emotional regulation and how we react when we feel certain emotions, so the movie is a good way to sum up what we have talked about.
  • It also gives students a nice, entertaining break.
  • If we do not finish the movie, we will finish it next class.

Closure

  • At the end of class, I pause the movie at a good stopping point if we have not finished. I remind students that we will finish it next class.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: We finish Inside Out 2 if we did not finish today.
