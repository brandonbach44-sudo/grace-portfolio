Student-Led Communication Email (weekly Thursday)

ESSThursdayLessonPlan.docx

← Friday Routine: Missing Work & Organization
RecurringOrganization & Self-AdvocacyEssential Skills and Strategies (ESS)

PA Standards

PA Career Education and Work Standard 13.3.11.B: Analyze the role of self-advocacy and professional communication skills in personal and academic success.

Essential Questions

  • How do I write a clear, professional email to communicate with important people in my life?
  • Why is self-advocacy, sharing my strengths and areas I want to grow in, important for my success?

Objectives

  • Students will write a student-led email to their case manager, parent(s), and ESS teacher using the provided template.
  • Students will include a subject line, greeting, body (one strength and one growth area), and a screenshot of their PowerSchool grades in their email.
  • Students will use a word bank to compose an appropriate greeting and closing.
  • Students will proofread their email using a provided checklist before sending.
  • Students will organize their inbox by deleting old emails or replying to any that need a response.

Assessment

  • Completed, sent email checked against template requirements (subject line, greeting, body with strength/growth area, grade screenshot)
  • Completed proofreading checklist
  • Completed inbox organization checklist
  • Progress on missing assignments during remaining work time

Differentiation

  • Physical copy and online/digital copy of the email template both provided so students can use whichever format supports them best
  • Word bank provided for greeting and closing language
  • Proofreading checklist scaffolds self-editing (subject line present, greeting/closing used, screenshot attached, etc.)
  • Inbox organization checklist scaffolds the closing routine (delete old emails, reply to any needing a response)
  • Teacher/aide support available for typing, taking the PowerSchool screenshot, or navigating email

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Visual schedule displayed showing today's routine
  • Consistent daily routine: check-in → warm-up → student-led email → inbox organization → missing work time
  • Clear expectations reviewed for professional/appropriate use of school email
  • Positive reinforcement for completing the self-advocacy email independently

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • (0-5 min) Students enter and complete their daily check-in.
  • (5-20 min) Warm-up group activity: Wordle, Contexto, or the Sets game.

Materials Needed

  • Visual schedule
  • Daily check-in sheet or digital tool
  • Wordle/Contexto/Sets game
  • Physical copy of the student-led email template
  • Online/digital copy of the student-led email template
  • Word bank handout (greetings and closings)
  • Email proofreading checklist
  • Inbox organization checklist
  • Devices with access to student email (starred email to reply all to) and PowerSchool
  • Individual missing-assignment list per student

Vocabulary

  • Self-advocacy — speaking up for your own needs and progress
  • Subject line — a short summary of what an email is about
  • Greeting/closing — the opening and ending of an email
  • Proofread — checking your work carefully for mistakes before submitting

Lesson Sequence

  • (20-50 min) Students draft their student-led email using the physical or online template, replying all to their starred email. Email includes: a subject line, a greeting (using the word bank), a body with one thing they are proud of and one thing they want to work on, and a screenshot of their current PowerSchool grades.
  • (50-60 min) Students proofread their email using the checklist (subject line, greeting/closing, screenshot attached, etc.) and send it.
  • (60-70 min) Students use the inbox organization checklist to delete old emails or reply to any that need a response.
  • (70-90 min) Students work on any missing assignments

Closure

  • Quick check-out: did you send your email? How do you feel about the strength and growth area you shared?
  • Preview that next week will follow the same daily routine.
