11/16
Monday: Making Decisions Slides: https://canva.link/jb88o2fcdfn48fs Daily Check-In Mindfulness Monday meditation video Group activity: this or that Introduce lesson on making decisions Play video on making decisions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?si=RXuc-Y3sy97HDa3Q&v=pPIhAm_WGbQ&feature=youtu.be Discuss video: When making decisions…. It is important to consider your options when making important decisions Step 1 is to identify the problem Decide what is most important to you Make a pros and cons list of both your options Consider the consequences: remember consequences can be good or bad, they can also happen later (not right away) If you choose the wrong job or apartment, you may not realize it right away Pretend you are helping someone; seeing things from a different perspective can make it easier to help others, pretend you are helping someone else: what would you say to someone in your shoes? Remember to take your time and think when making big decisions Overall: Making decisions can be hard It can be overwhelming thinking of all the options to choose from, try to narrow your choices down by deciding what you definitely don't want to do It is okay to feel frustrated when making decisions Use a coping strategy Take a break from the situation, taking a break can help you clear your mind and may help you decide faster Think about your desired outcome and all consequences before making a decision If your desired outcome is to pass your test, your decision should be to stay home and study/complete your work instead of scrolling on your phone or watching tv (it’s okay to do these things as a break) It is okay to change your mind/grow We change as we grow and decisions we make before may change, that is okay! You are allowed to change your mind, it happens to everyone.
