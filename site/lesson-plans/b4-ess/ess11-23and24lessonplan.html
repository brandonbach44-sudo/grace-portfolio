11/23Essential Skills and Strategies

PA Standards

  • Skills: Self-awareness and emotional regulation: identify emotions, how we react to them, and the consequences of our actions.
  • Skills: Communication: share ideas and listen during a class discussion.

Essential Questions

  • How do my emotions affect the way I act?
  • What can happen, good or bad, because of how I react to my emotions?
  • How can recognizing my emotions before I act help me make a different choice?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to:
  • Name the emotions they saw in Inside Out 2 and how the characters reacted when they felt them.
  • Describe good and bad consequences of those actions.
  • Explain what the main character was feeling and why, and how she could change her actions if she recognized her emotions before she acted.
  • Use the remaining time to finish missing assignments.

Assessment

  • Formative: Student participation and responses in the class discussion about emotions, reactions, and consequences.
  • Formative: Student responses about what the main character was feeling and how she could change her actions.
  • Practice data: Missing assignments completed.

Differentiation

  • Content: The movie uses pictures and characters to show emotions, which helps students connect to what we have discussed.
  • Process: The discussion questions move from what happened to why it happened and what could be different.
  • Process: Students can share ideas out loud in the discussion.
  • Choice: Students choose between finishing missing assignments and playing games after the discussion.
  • IEP/504: Accommodations in each student’s IEP/504 plan will be followed.

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Class is shortened to 45 minutes instead of the usual hour and 15 minutes.
  • We finish Inside Out 2 first, then have a class discussion.
  • Students take turns sharing ideas and listening to classmates.
  • After the discussion, students may finish missing assignments or play games.

Activating Prior Knowledge

Students have been learning about emotional regulation and how we react when we feel certain emotions. Finishing Inside Out 2 and talking about it gives them a real example to connect to what we have learned.

Materials Needed

Inside Out 2, projector/screen, discussion questions, missing assignments, games

Vocabulary

Emotion; emotional regulation; reaction; consequence; recognize; choice; action

Lesson Sequence

  • Class Period — 45 minutes
  • Class is shorter today, 45 minutes instead of an hour and 15 minutes.
  • Finish Inside Out 2
  • We finish watching Inside Out 2.
  • Class Discussion
  • We talk about the emotions we saw.
  • We talk about how the characters reacted when they felt these emotions.
  • We talk about whether there were any consequences, good or bad, from these actions.
  • We discuss what the main character was feeling and why.
  • We discuss how she could change her actions if she recognized her emotions before she acted.
  • Missing Assignments or Games
  • Students have time to finish missing assignments or play games.

Closure

  • At the end of the discussion, I ask students to share one way recognizing an emotion before acting could help them in their own lives. Then students move on to missing assignments or games.
  • Connection to Next Lesson: Students keep using strategies for recognizing and managing their emotions.
