11/18
Wednesday: Asking Questions Slides: https://youtu.be/ko7NRPhxlTA?si=t-FGcXOTxhFMDrBH Daily Check-In Group Activity 21 Questions Have each student choose an item from the cards given Other students need to ask them yes or no questions to find out Video on why asking questions is important: https://youtu.be/qQBXyoDzdiM?si=C4BaZ3l5xop5Ly7a Discuss: Asking questions shows that you’re listening It continues conversations: Asking questions about a topic helps to fill any gaps in conversation so that it can continue It can clear any confusion: asking questions helps you make sure you know what the conversation is about so you can actively contribute At school it can help you understand a topic or project that is confusing or new to you At work, you can ask questions before or during an interview to get a better idea about what you will be doing at work You can also ask questions when you don’t understand a job you are supposed to be doing so that you make sure you do it correct You can learn new things by asking questions: other people have knowledge you haven't learned yet or perspectives you have never considered. This expands your knowledge and makes you more open and interesting to other people. Overall: Asking questions helps you form and maintain relationships with people When you ask questions you get to know people and what they like It also helps you understand a person and their perspective which is a great tool when forming friendships It helps you make sure you understand the conversation or a job/assignment It is fun to learn new things Work on missing assingments
