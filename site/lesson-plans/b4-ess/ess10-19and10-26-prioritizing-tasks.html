10/19, 10/26
Monday Prioritizing Tasks Daily Check-In Group Question: What is your favorite Halloween movie? Mindfulness Introduce prioritizing tasks lesson What does it mean to prioritize tasks? Let students answer with their ideas Prioritizing tasks means to arrange or complete tasks in the order of importance and urgency (more important tasks come first) What tasks should you be prioritizing? Let students answer with their ideas (let them known its okay if they don't know) We will talk more about what tasks should be prioritized first Show prioritizing tasks video (start at 1:30 min) The video mentions splitting tasks into “urgent” and “not urgent”, how do you decide what is urgent vs what is not? Urgent: the due date is soon, it is worth a lot of points (tests, projects), it is a meeting/interview that is soon Prioritizing tasks slide: Again, decide what is urgent and what is important Consider what your goal is: It is important to complete your goals, however it is also important to decide whether or not your goal can wait, is there something you need to complete first? Examples from the video: the girl’s goal was to finish her video game, but she recognized that she needed to pack for her trip that was the next day before she could finish her video game Next Slide: BIG, medium, small tasks Split your tasks into BIG, medium, and small tasks Big tasks come FIRST: exams, big projects, job interviews Medium tasks come second, they should still get done, but consider doing them after your big tasks: daily homework, club meetings Small tasks: you should still be doing small tasks and fun activities, but try to complete your important work first because these things are not a priority. Hand out prioritizing work worksheet, students can fill in their tasks in the calendar and answer the question below based on the lesson and their big vs small tasks Give time to finish missing assignments
