Active Listening & Conversation Skills

ESS10-7&10-14LessonPlan.docx

← Triggers & Coping StrategiesStaying Organized: Keeping an Agenda →
10/07, 10/14Social & Emotional SkillsSocial Skills

PA Standards

PA SEL Competency — Relationship Skills: Use communication and social skills to interact effectively with others, including active listening and turn-taking in conversation.

Essential Questions

  • What does it look like to actively listen and keep a conversation going?
  • How can I use conversation skills to build and maintain connections with others?

Objectives

  • Students will be able to identify at least two behaviors of active listening (e.g., eye contact, nodding, asking follow-up questions).
  • Students will be able to practice starting and maintaining a conversation using a shared prompt.
  • Students will be able to take turns respectfully during a group conversation activity.

Assessment

  • Participation in the group discussion on active listening/conversation skills
  • Teacher observation during Conversation Jenga: turn-taking, engagement, use of active listening behaviors

Differentiation

  • Visual list/anchor chart of active listening behaviors posted for reference during the activity
  • Option to pass on a question and choose a different block
  • Sentence starters available for students who need support beginning a response
  • Small, consistent group sizes for Conversation Jenga to reduce social pressure
  • Extra processing time allowed before a student is expected to respond

Classroom Environment & Routines

  • Consistent daily routine: warm-up → check-in → group discussion → Conversation Jenga → closure
  • Clear group norms posted for Conversation Jenga: one person talks at a time, listen while others answer, 'pass' is always allowed
  • Small groups arranged ahead of time to support comfortable participation

Activating Prior Knowledge

  • (0-10 min) Warm-up: Wordle, Contexto, or the Sets game as a whole group.
  • (10-15 min) Daily check-in.

Materials Needed

  • Wordle/Contexto/Sets game (board or device access)
  • Daily check-in sheet or digital tool
  • Active listening anchor chart/visual
  • Conversation Jenga tower(s) with conversation-starter questions on each block

Vocabulary

  • Active listening — fully focusing on and responding to what someone is saying
  • Turn-taking — waiting for your turn to speak and letting others speak without interrupting
  • Follow-up question — a question that shows interest and keeps a conversation going

Lesson Sequence

  • (15-25 min) Group discussion: introduce active listening and conversation skills. Discuss what active listening looks like (eye contact, nodding, asking follow-up questions) and why it matters for keeping a conversation going. Model a short example conversation together, pointing out these behaviors.
  • (25-55 min) Conversation Jenga: in small groups, students take turns pulling a block, reading the question aloud, and answering it while practicing active listening (e.g., one other group member asks a follow-up question before the next turn). Teacher circulates to support group and reinforce active listening behaviors.
  • (55-85 min) Catch-up work time: students use remaining class time to complete missing assignments or other classwork, with teacher support available.

Closure

(85-90 min) Groups clean up their Jenga towers. Briefly recap: what's one active listening behavior you used or noticed today? Preview that tomorrow will continue practicing conversation/social skills.
