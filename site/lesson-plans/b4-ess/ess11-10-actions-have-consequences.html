Slides: https://canva.link/28hjru19k9zbhm7 Your Actions have Consequences Daily check-in Ask students to think and share: Ask students: "Have you ever made a decision and later wished you had made a different choice?" What is a Consequence: Explain: A consequence is the result of something you do or say. Every action can lead to an outcome. Consequences can be positive or negative. Sometimes consequences happen immediately, and sometimes they happen later. Examples: Completing your homework → feeling prepared for class. Being disrespectful → damaging a relationship. Helping someone → building a friendship. How choices affect YOU (Wednesday's social skills lesson will be about how your actions affect others) Your Choices Affect YOU Discuss how our actions can affect our own lives. School: Turning in assignments can help you stay on track. Friendships: Being kind can help build trust. Responsibilities: Being organized can help you become more independent. Opportunities: Making responsible choices can help you reach your goals. Ask: "What is one choice you can make today that will help you in the future?" What Could Happen? (Real-Life Scenarios) Present a few situations and have students identify the action and possible consequences. You are talking to a friend during class instead of completing your assignment. You forget to write an important assignment in your agenda. A teacher asks you to put your phone away, but you refuse. You notice a classmate needs help, so you offer assistance. You are frustrated with someone and respond rudely. Ask students: What is happening? What choice is the person making? What could happen next? What could the person do differently? Remind students that it is okay to make mistakes, but using coping strategies and stopping to think before they act is important when it comes to avoiding negative consequences in the future Work on missing assignments
